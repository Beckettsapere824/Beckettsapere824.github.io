Looking for co-founders for Noesyx Lab.

We are exploring the cognitive path of Human → AI → Computer — to free people from repetitive labor and obsolete forms of education. Agents should not live on a laptop. They should live in the brain.

AI will not replace humans. It will push us into an unprecedented era: one where the volume of knowledge and creative output exceeds the sum of all matter on Earth, and eventually the universe. In the coming decades, every individual will interface with AI to process information, and will interact, through chips, with global networks, robots, databases and computers. Within 70 years, universities and schools will disappear. Knowledge and skills will become modular, plug-and-play tools. The real danger is monopoly over those tools. Noesyx Lab was founded to prevent that — to let AI become a second brain of human beings, and to ensure every individual can truly benefit from this technological revolution.

Humans excel at construction, not memory; at creation, not following procedures. AI is converging with us on this point, while what it uniquely enables is the bridge from mind to machine. We cannot yet discuss how the next century will price creativity — that question has not arrived. What we can do, however, is to critique the modern education and excessive specialization produced by industrial civilization. Modern people are wealthier. They are not happier.

People fear AI will take their jobs. We never wanted jobs. We wanted to dream. AI does not dream. Not yet.

I am also open to meeting more founders, co-founders, engineers and researchers — not only those whose directions perfectly overlap with ours. As co-founder of Zenno-X, we spent the past two months studying and building methodologies that actually help early teams grow: hackathons, workshops, sustained support systems. Now we want to strengthen ties with people around the world and explore more efficient ways to advance AI entrepreneurship and deep tech progress, while building a community of real mutual value.

If any of this resonates, message me directly. Extra content in previous posts.
