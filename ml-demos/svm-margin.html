What you should notice

  • Only the points that touch the margin (support vectors) determine the hyperplane.
  • Moving a non-support vector does nothing to the boundary.
  • Moving a support vector immediately rotates / shifts the slab.
  • When classes become non-separable the solver reports failure (hard-margin cannot exist).
