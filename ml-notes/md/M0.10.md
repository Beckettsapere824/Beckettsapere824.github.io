# M0.10 Unconstrained Optimality Condition ($\nabla f=0$)

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.8 Matrix calculus](M0.8.md), [M0.9 Convexity](M0.9.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

We can compute gradients. When does “set the gradient to zero” actually give the solution we want?

会算梯度了。「令梯度为零」何时真的给出想要的解？

---

## Step 1: Necessary condition / 第一步：必要条件

If $f$ is differentiable and $\mathbf{x}^{*}$ is a **local** minimiser in the interior of the domain, then

若 $f$ 可微且 $\mathbf{x}^{*}$ 是区域内部的 **局部** 最小点，则

$$
\nabla f(\mathbf{x}^{*})=\mathbf{0}.
$$

Otherwise a step opposite the gradient would decrease $f$.

否则沿负梯度走一步会使 $f$ 下降。

---

## Step 2: Sufficient when convex / 第二步：凸时充分

If $f$ is convex and $\nabla f(\mathbf{x}^{*})=\mathbf{0}$, then $\mathbf{x}^{*}$ is a **global** minimiser (M0.9).

若 $f$ 凸且 $\nabla f(\mathbf{x}^{*})=\mathbf{0}$，则 $\mathbf{x}^{*}$ 是 **全局** 最小点（M0.9）。

---

## Step 3: Least squares walkthrough / 第三步：最小二乘走一遍

$$
J(\mathbf{w})=\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}\rVert_{2}^{2},
\quad
\nabla J=X^{\top}(X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}).
$$

Set $\nabla J=\mathbf{0}$ → **normal equations** $X^{\top}X\,\mathbf{w}=X^{\top}\mathbf{y}$.  
$J$ is convex (Hessian $X^{\top}X$ is PSD). If $X$ has full column rank, the solution is unique and global.

令梯度为零 → **正规方程**。$J$ 凸（Hessian $X^{\top}X$ 半正定）。$X$ 满列秩时解唯一且全局。

---

## Step 4: Non-convex warning / 第四步：非凸警告

Deep nets have many stationary points; some are saddles or poor local minima. $\nabla f=\mathbf{0}$ is then only necessary, not a certificate of global optimality.

深度网络有许多驻点；有的是鞍点或差的局部最小。此时 $\nabla f=\mathbf{0}$ 只是必要，不是全局最优的证明。

---

## Practice / 练习

Why does adding $\tfrac{\lambda}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}$ with $\lambda>0$ make the ridge objective strictly convex when $X^{\top}X$ alone may not be?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Hessian becomes $X^{\top}X+\lambda I$, whose eigenvalues are at least $\lambda>0$, hence PD.

Hessian 变为 $X^{\top}X+\lambda I$，特征值 $\ge\lambda>0$，故正定。

</details>
