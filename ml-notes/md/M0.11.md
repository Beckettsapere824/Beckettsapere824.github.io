# M0.11 Equality-Constrained Optimisation & Lagrange Multipliers

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.10 $\nabla f=0$](M0.10.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Hard-margin SVM minimises $\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}$ subject to equality-like constraints on the margin. Unconstrained $\nabla f=\mathbf{0}$ is not enough—we must stay on the constraint surface.

硬间隔 SVM 在间隔约束下最小化 $\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}$。无约束的 $\nabla f=\mathbf{0}$ 不够——必须停在约束曲面上。

---

## Step 1: Geometry / 第一步：几何

Minimise $f(\mathbf{w})$ subject to $h(\mathbf{w})=0$. At an optimum, you cannot decrease $f$ by moving **along** the surface $h=0$. That requires $\nabla f$ to be parallel to $\nabla h$:

在 $h(\mathbf{w})=0$ 下最小化 $f$。最优点处，沿约束面移动不能再降 $f$，故 $\nabla f$ 必须与 $\nabla h$ **平行**：

$$
\nabla f(\mathbf{w})=\lambda\,\nabla h(\mathbf{w})
\quad\text{for some scalar }\lambda.
$$

---

## Step 2: Lagrangian / 第二步：拉格朗日函数

Define

定义

$$
L(\mathbf{w},\lambda)=f(\mathbf{w})+\lambda h(\mathbf{w}).
$$

Stationarity $\nabla_{\mathbf{w}}L=\mathbf{0}$ and $\nabla_{\lambda}L=0$ recover the parallel-gradient condition and the constraint $h=0$.

对 $\mathbf{w}$ 与 $\lambda$ 的驻点条件分别恢复梯度共线与约束 $h=0$。

For several equalities $h_{i}(\mathbf{w})=0$, use $L=f+\sum_{i}\lambda_{i}h_{i}$.

多个等式时 $L=f+\sum_{i}\lambda_{i}h_{i}$。

---

## Step 3: Worked example / 第三步：完整小例子

Minimise $f(x,y)=x^{2}+y^{2}$ on the circle $h(x,y)=x^{2}+y^{2}-1=0$.  
$L=x^{2}+y^{2}+\lambda(x^{2}+y^{2}-1)$.  
$\partial L/\partial x=2x+2\lambda x=0$, $\partial L/\partial y=2y+2\lambda y=0$, $x^{2}+y^{2}=1$.  
Solutions: all points on the unit circle with $\lambda=-1$, and $f=1$ constantly on the constraint—consistent (every feasible point is optimal).

在单位圆上最小化 $x^{2}+y^{2}$：可行点上 $f\equiv 1$，与 $\lambda=-1$ 的驻点一致。

A less degenerate case: minimise $x+y$ on $x^{2}+y^{2}=1$. Then $\nabla f=(1,1)^{\top}$ aligns with $\nabla h=2(x,y)^{\top}$ at $\pm(1,1)/\sqrt{2}$; the minimum is $-\sqrt{2}$.

更典型：在圆上最小化 $x+y$，最优点在 $-(1,1)/\sqrt{2}$，最优值 $-\sqrt{2}$。

---

## Step 4: Bridge to SVM / 第四步：接到 SVM

The hard-margin primal is a quadratic objective with linear inequality constraints; forming the Lagrangian and eliminating $\mathbf{w}$ produces the dual that depends only on inner products—start of the kernel story (M3).

硬间隔原问题是带线性不等式的二次目标；写 Lagrangian 并消去 $\mathbf{w}$ 得到只依赖内积的对偶——核方法的起点（M3）。

---

## Practice / 练习

State the Lagrangian for $\min_{\mathbf{w}}\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}$ s.t. $\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}=1$ for $i=1,\ldots,m$ (toy equalities).

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$L(\mathbf{w},\boldsymbol{\lambda})=\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}+\sum_{i}\lambda_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}-1)$.

</details>
