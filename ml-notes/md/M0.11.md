# M0.11 Lagrange Multipliers

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.10](M0.10.md)

---

## Geometry / 几何

On $h(\mathbf{w})=0$, optimum requires $\nabla f \parallel \nabla h$:

$$
\nabla f = \lambda \nabla h.
$$

## Lagrangian / 拉格朗日

$$
L(\mathbf{w},\lambda)=f(\mathbf{w})+\lambda h(\mathbf{w}).
$$

Stationarity recovers parallel gradients and the constraint.

驻点条件恢复梯度共线与约束。

## Example / 例子

Minimise $x+y$ on $x^{2}+y^{2}=1$: optimum at $-(1,1)/\sqrt{2}$, value $-\sqrt{2}$.

## SVM bridge / 接 SVM

Lagrangian of hard-margin primal leads to dual depending only on inner products.

硬间隔 Lagrangian 导向只依赖内积的对偶。
