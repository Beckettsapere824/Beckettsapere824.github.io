# M0.12 Inequality Constraints & KKT Conditions (preview)

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.11 Lagrange](M0.11.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Soft-margin SVM allows points to violate the margin with a penalty. Constraints become inequalities. Which multipliers are active? **KKT** conditions organise the answer; this node is a preview so SVM dual bounds $0\le\alpha_{i}\le C$ look familiar rather than magical.

软间隔 SVM 允许点以惩罚方式越过间隔，约束变成不等式。哪些乘子起作用？**KKT** 条件整理答案；本节点是预告，使 SVM 对偶里 $0\le\alpha_{i}\le C$ 显得自然而不是魔法。

---

## Step 1: Inequalities and the boundary / 第一步：不等式与边界

Minimise $f(\mathbf{w})$ subject to $g(\mathbf{w})\le 0$.  
The optimum may lie in the **interior** ($g<0$), where the constraint is inactive, or on the **boundary** ($g=0$), where it binds.

在 $g(\mathbf{w})\le 0$ 下最小化 $f$。最优点可能在 **内部**（$g<0$，约束不起作用）或 **边界**（$g=0$，约束贴紧）。

---

## Step 2: Complementary slackness / 第二步：互补松弛

Associate a multiplier $\alpha\ge 0$ with $g\le 0$. **Complementary slackness** says

给 $g\le 0$ 配乘子 $\alpha\ge 0$。**互补松弛** 说

$$
\alpha\, g(\mathbf{w})=0.
$$

So either $\alpha=0$ (constraint inactive) or $g=0$ (on the boundary). You never need a positive multiplier for a slack constraint.

要么 $\alpha=0$（约束不活跃），要么 $g=0$（在边界上）。松的约束不需要正乘子。

---

## Step 3: KKT checklist (recognition level) / 第三步：KKT 清单（认人水平）

For a problem with inequalities $g_{i}\le 0$ and equalities $h_{j}=0$, a regular optimum satisfies:

带不等式 $g_{i}\le 0$ 与等式 $h_{j}=0$ 时，正则最优点满足：

1. **Stationarity:** $\nabla f+\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}\nabla g_{i}+\sum_{j}\lambda_{j}\nabla h_{j}=\mathbf{0}$  
2. **Primal feasibility:** $g_{i}\le 0$, $h_{j}=0$  
3. **Dual feasibility:** $\alpha_{i}\ge 0$  
4. **Complementary slackness:** $\alpha_{i}g_{i}=0$

You do not need to derive these from scratch in this course; you need to **recognise** them when SVM notes write $0\le\alpha_{i}\le C$.

本课不必从零推导；要在 SVM 笔记写出 $0\le\alpha_{i}\le C$ 时能够 **认出来**。

---

## Step 4: Soft-margin SVM preview / 第四步：软间隔预告

Slack variables $\xi_{i}\ge 0$ turn hard constraints into soft ones; the dual multipliers for the margin constraints end up boxed in $[0,C]$. Support vectors correspond to points that sit on or across the margin—precisely where complementary slackness allows $\alpha_{i}>0$.

松弛变量 $\xi_{i}\ge 0$ 把硬约束变软；间隔约束的对偶乘子落在 $[0,C]$。支持向量对应贴边或越界的点——恰是互补松弛允许 $\alpha_{i}>0$ 之处。

---

## Practice / 练习

In one sentence: what does complementary slackness forbid?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

A strictly inactive inequality ($g_{i}<0$) cannot carry a positive multiplier ($\alpha_{i}>0$).

严格不活跃的不等式（$g_{i}<0$）不能带有正乘子（$\alpha_{i}>0$）。

</details>
