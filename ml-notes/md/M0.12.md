# M0.12 KKT Conditions (preview)

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.11](M0.11.md)

---

## Complementary slackness / 互补松弛

For $g(\mathbf{w})\le 0$ with multiplier $\alpha\ge 0$:

$$
\alpha\, g(\mathbf{w})=0.
$$

Either constraint inactive ($\alpha=0$) or on boundary ($g=0$).

要么约束不活跃，要么在边界上。

## KKT checklist / 清单

1. Stationarity  
2. Primal feasibility  
3. Dual feasibility $\alpha_{i}\ge 0$  
4. Complementary slackness

## Soft-margin preview / 软间隔

Dual multipliers boxed in $[0,C]$; support vectors are points with $\alpha_{i}>0$.

对偶乘子在 $[0,C]$；支持向量对应 $\alpha_{i}>0$。
