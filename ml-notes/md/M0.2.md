# M0.2 Norms

Distance induced by a norm: $d(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})=\lVert \mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y} \rVert$.

由范数诱导的距离：$d(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})=\lVert \mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y} \rVert$。

$$
\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert _{1} = \sum_{i} |x_{i}|,\qquad
\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert _{2} = \sqrt{\sum_{i} x_{i}^{2}},\qquad
\lVert \mathbf{x} \rVert _{\infty} = \max_{i} |x_{i}|.
$$
