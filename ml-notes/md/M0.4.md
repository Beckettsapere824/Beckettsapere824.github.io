# M0.4 Rank, Column Space, Null Space, Invertibility

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3](M0.3.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

When does $X^{\top}X\,\mathbf{w}=X^{\top}\mathbf{y}$ have a unique solution? When columns of $X$ are linearly independent.

$X^{\top}X\,\mathbf{w}=X^{\top}\mathbf{y}$ 何时唯一解？当 $X$ 列线性无关。

---

## Column space and null space / 列空间与零空间

- $\mathrm{Col}(A)$: all $A\mathbf{x}$.  
- $\mathrm{Null}(A)=\{\mathbf{x}:A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{0}\}$.

Rank–nullity: $\mathrm{rank}(A)+\dim\mathrm{Null}(A)=n$ for $A\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$.

Full column rank $\Leftrightarrow$ $\mathrm{Null}(A)=\{\mathbf{0}\}$.

---

## Link to $X^{\top}X$

$\mathrm{Null}(X^{\top}X)=\mathrm{Null}(X)$, because $X^{\top}X\mathbf{w}=\mathbf{0}\Rightarrow\lVert X\mathbf{w}\rVert _{2}^{2}=0\Rightarrow X\mathbf{w}=\mathbf{0}$.

So $X^{\top}X$ invertible iff $X$ has full column rank.

---

## Example

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\\3&6\end{pmatrix}
$$

has rank 1; $A^{\top}A$ singular.
