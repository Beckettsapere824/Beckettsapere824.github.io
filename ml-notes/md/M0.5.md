# M0.5 Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors, Spectral Decomposition

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.4 Rank](M0.4.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

A matrix mixes directions: some stretch, some shrink, some rotate. For **symmetric** matrices the picture simplifies—there exist special axes that the matrix only scales, never twists. Those axes are the backbone of PCA.

矩阵会混合方向：有的拉长、有的压短、有的旋转。对 **对称** 矩阵，存在只被缩放、不被扭转的特殊轴。这些轴是 PCA 的骨架。

---

## Step 1: The defining equation / 第一步：定义方程

A nonzero vector $\mathbf{v}$ is an **eigenvector** of $A$ with **eigenvalue** $\lambda$ if

非零向量 $\mathbf{v}$ 是 $A$ 的 **特征向量**，对应 **特征值** $\lambda$，当且仅当

$$
A\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}.
$$

Geometrically: applying $A$ only stretches or flips $\mathbf{v}$; direction stays on the same line.

几何上：乘 $A$ 只把 $\mathbf{v}$ 伸缩或反向，不离开它所在直线。

**2D picture / 二维图景.** Think of an ellipse. The long axis and short axis are the directions that stay aligned under the linear map that turns the unit circle into that ellipse—those are the eigenvector directions; the stretch factors are the eigenvalues.

想象椭圆：长轴与短轴在「单位圆变成椭圆」的线性映射下保持对齐——它们就是特征向量方向，伸缩倍数就是特征值。

---

## Step 2: Symmetric matrices are special / 第二步：对称矩阵特别好

If $A=A^{\top}$ (real symmetric), then:

若 $A=A^{\top}$（实对称），则：

1. All eigenvalues are **real**. / 特征值全是 **实数**。  
2. Eigenvectors for distinct eigenvalues are **orthogonal**. / 不同特征值的特征向量 **正交**。  
3. There exists an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors:  
   存在标准正交的特征基：

$$
A = Q\Lambda Q^{\top},
$$

where $Q$ is orthogonal ($Q^{\top}Q=I$) and $\Lambda=\mathrm{diag}(\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{n})$. This is the **spectral decomposition**.

其中 $Q$ 正交，$\Lambda$ 对角。这叫 **谱分解**。

Not every matrix admits an orthogonal eigenbasis—symmetry is the key hypothesis you will use for covariance matrices in PCA.

不是每个矩阵都有正交特征基——对称性是 PCA 里协方差矩阵能谱分解的关键假设。

---

## Step 3: Tiny hand computation / 第三步：手算小例子

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Characteristic equation $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$:

特征方程 $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$：

$$
(2-\lambda)^{2}-1=0 \Rightarrow \lambda=3\ \text{or}\ 1.
$$

For $\lambda=3$: $(A-3I)\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{0}$ gives $v_{1}=v_{2}$, e.g. $\mathbf{v}_{1}=(1,1)^{\top}/\sqrt{2}$.  
For $\lambda=1$: $v_{1}=-v_{2}$, e.g. $\mathbf{v}_{2}=(1,-1)^{\top}/\sqrt{2}$.

They are orthogonal, as promised.

二者正交，符合对称矩阵的性质。

---

## Step 4: Rayleigh quotient (why PCA maximises variance) / 第四步：Rayleigh 商

For symmetric $A$ and $\mathbf{u}\neq\mathbf{0}$,

$$
R(\mathbf{u})=\frac{\mathbf{u}^{\top}A\mathbf{u}}{\mathbf{u}^{\top}\mathbf{u}}.
$$

The maximum of $R$ is the largest eigenvalue; the minimiser direction is the corresponding eigenvector. PCA will set $A$ to the covariance and maximise projected variance—exactly this Rayleigh problem.

$R$ 的最大值是最大特征值，方向是对应特征向量。PCA 把 $A$ 取成协方差、最大化投影方差——正是这个问题。

---

## Practice / 练习

For $A=\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, what are the eigenpairs? Which direction maximises $\mathbf{u}^{\top}A\mathbf{u}$ on the unit circle?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$\lambda=3$ with $\mathbf{e}_{1}=(1,0)^{\top}$; $\lambda=1$ with $\mathbf{e}_{2}=(0,1)^{\top}$. Maximum of $\mathbf{u}^{\top}A\mathbf{u}$ on $\lVert\mathbf{u}\rVert_{2}=1$ is $3$, along $\mathbf{e}_{1}$.

</details>
