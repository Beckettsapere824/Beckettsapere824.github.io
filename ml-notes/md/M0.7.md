# M0.7 Partial Derivatives and Gradient of a Scalar Function

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.1 Vectors](M0.1.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Training almost every model means “make a loss smaller.” In many parameters, which way is downhill? The **gradient** packs every directional slope into one vector that points to the steepest ascent (so minus the gradient is steepest descent).

训练几乎所有模型都是「让损失变小」。参数很多时，哪边是下坡？**梯度**把各个方向的斜率收成一个向量，指向最陡上升（负梯度即最陡下降）。

---

## Step 1: From one variable to many / 第一步：从一元到多元

For $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$, the derivative $f'(x)$ is the slope.  
For $f:\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathbb{R}$, fix all coordinates but $x_{j}$ and differentiate: that is the **partial derivative** $\partial f/\partial x_{j}$.

一元导数是斜率。多元时固定其他坐标只对 $x_{j}$ 求导，得到 **偏导数** $\partial f/\partial x_{j}$。

---

## Step 2: The gradient vector / 第二步：梯度向量

$$
\nabla f(\mathbf{x})
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\partial f/\partial x_{1} \\ \vdots \\ \partial f/\partial x_{d}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Example / 例子.** $f(x,y)=x^{2}+3xy$. Then $\nabla f=(2x+3y,\,3x)^{\top}$. At $(1,1)$ the gradient is $(5,3)^{\top}$.

---

## Step 3: Directional derivative / 第三步：方向导数

Along a unit vector $\mathbf{u}$, the rate of change is

沿单位向量 $\mathbf{u}$ 的变化率为

$$
D_{\mathbf{u}}f = \nabla f^{\top}\mathbf{u} = \lVert\nabla f\rVert_{2}\cos\theta.
$$

This is maximised when $\mathbf{u}$ points the same way as $\nabla f$. Hence: **gradient = direction of steepest ascent**.

当 $\mathbf{u}$ 与 $\nabla f$ 同向时最大。因此：**梯度 = 最陡上升方向**。

---

## Step 4: Link to learning algorithms / 第四步：接到学习算法

Gradient descent updates $\mathbf{w}\leftarrow\mathbf{w}-\eta\nabla J(\mathbf{w})$: a small step opposite the gradient lowers $J$ (for small enough $\eta$, when $J$ is smooth).

梯度下降：$\mathbf{w}\leftarrow\mathbf{w}-\eta\nabla J(\mathbf{w})$，沿负梯度走一小步使 $J$ 下降（$\eta$ 足够小且 $J$ 光滑时）。

---

## Practice / 练习

Compute $\nabla f$ for $f(\mathbf{w})=\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}$. What is the steepest-ascent direction at $\mathbf{w}=(3,4)^{\top}$?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$\nabla f(\mathbf{w})=\mathbf{w}$. At $(3,4)$ the direction is $(3,4)$ itself (or its unit vector $(3/5,4/5)$).

$\nabla f=\mathbf{w}$；在 $(3,4)$ 最陡上升就是该方向。

</details>
