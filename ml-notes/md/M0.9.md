# M0.9 Convex Sets, Convex Functions, First-order Characterisation

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.7 Gradients](M0.7.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

People say “convex problems are easy.” Easy in what sense? Because a local minimum is automatically global—there are no false valleys. We need a precise test for that property.

常说「凸问题好算」。好在哪？因为局部最小自动是全局最小——没有假山谷。需要一个可操作的判定。

---

## Step 1: Convex set / 第一步：凸集

A set $C$ is **convex** if for any $\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}\in C$ and $t\in[0,1]$,

集合 $C$ 是 **凸集**，若任意 $\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}\in C$ 与 $t\in[0,1]$，

$$
t\mathbf{x}+(1-t)\mathbf{y}\in C.
$$

The line segment between any two points stays inside. Half-spaces, balls, and probability simplices are convex; a crescent is not.

任意两点的连线段仍在集合内。半空间、球、概率单纯形是凸的；月牙形不是。

---

## Step 2: Convex function / 第二步：凸函数

$f$ is **convex** on a convex set if for all $\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}$ and $t\in[0,1]$,

$f$ 在凸集上 **凸**，若

$$
f(t\mathbf{x}+(1-t)\mathbf{y})\le t f(\mathbf{x})+(1-t)f(\mathbf{y}).
$$

The graph lies below its chords. $f(\mathbf{w})=\lVert X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}\rVert_{2}^{2}$ is convex; $-\log$ is convex on $(0,\infty)$; a deep neural net loss usually is **not**.

图像在弦的下方。平方损失凸；$-\log$ 在 $(0,\infty)$ 上凸；深度网络损失通常 **不** 凸。

---

## Step 3: First-order characterisation / 第三步：一阶刻画

If $f$ is differentiable, convexity is equivalent to

若 $f$ 可微，凸等价于

$$
f(\mathbf{y})\ge f(\mathbf{x})+\nabla f(\mathbf{x})^{\top}(\mathbf{y}-\mathbf{x})
\quad\text{for all }\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}.
$$

The function never goes below its tangent plane. **Corollary:** if $\nabla f(\mathbf{x}^{*})=\mathbf{0}$, then $f(\mathbf{y})\ge f(\mathbf{x}^{*})$ for every $\mathbf{y}$—so $\mathbf{x}^{*}$ is a **global** minimiser.

函数永不低于切平面。**推论：** 若 $\nabla f(\mathbf{x}^{*})=\mathbf{0}$，则处处 $f(\mathbf{y})\ge f(\mathbf{x}^{*})$——$\mathbf{x}^{*}$ 是 **全局** 最小点。

---

## Step 4: Why this matters in the course / 第四步：本课为何在乎

Least squares, logistic regression (cross-entropy), and the SVM dual are convex in their parameters. Gradient methods may be slow, but they are not hunting the wrong valley.

最小二乘、逻辑回归（交叉熵）、SVM 对偶对参数是凸的。梯度法可能慢，但不会掉进错误的山谷。

---

## Practice / 练习

Using the first-order inequality, argue that any stationary point of a differentiable convex $f$ is a global minimiser.

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

If $\nabla f(\mathbf{x}^{*})=\mathbf{0}$, the inequality collapses to $f(\mathbf{y})\ge f(\mathbf{x}^{*})$ for all $\mathbf{y}$.

梯度为零时一阶不等式变成 $f(\mathbf{y})\ge f(\mathbf{x}^{*})$。

</details>
