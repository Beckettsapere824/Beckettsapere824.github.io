## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Imagine you are an intern in the ER. A new patient arrives with only a symptom vector (temperature, heart rate, blood pressure, …). In the cabinet are hundreds of past cases with the same kind of numbers and a final diagnosis (e.g. flu vs pneumonia).

想象你是急诊室的实习医生。来了一个新病人，手里只有一张症状表（体温、心率、血压……）。柜子里锁着过去几百个病例，每个都有同样的症状数字和最终诊断（比如「流感」或「肺炎」）。

You have no time for a textbook. The most naive idea is:

你没有时间翻教科书，最朴素的想法是：

> **Find the historically most similar case and copy its diagnosis.**
>
> **找历史上「症状最像」的那一个病人，看他得了什么病，就猜新病人也是这个病。**

That idea is **1-nearest neighbor (1-NN)**.

这就是 **1-最近邻（1-NN）**。

---

## Step 1: What does “similar” mean? / 第一步：什么叫「像」？

| Name / 名称 | Formula (2D) / 公式 |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Euclidean $\ell_{2}$ | $\sqrt{(x_{1}-z_{1})^{2}+(x_{2}-z_{2})^{2}}$ |
| Manhattan $\ell_{1}$ | $\lvert x_{1}-z_{1}\rvert+\lvert x_{2}-z_{2}\rvert$ |
| Chebyshev $\ell_{\infty}$ | $\max(\lvert x_{1}-z_{1}\rvert,\lvert x_{2}-z_{2}\rvert)$ |

Changing the ruler can change who is nearest.

换尺子可能改变谁最近。

---

## Step 2: 1-NN rule / 第二步：规则

$$
\hat{y}(\mathbf{x}) = y_{i^{*}},\qquad
i^{*} = \arg\min_{i}\, d(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{x}_{i}).
$$

No training phase—store data and scan at prediction time.

无训练阶段；预测时查库。

---

## Step 3: Voronoi / 第三步：Voronoi

Decision regions are cells of points closer to one training point than to any other; boundaries are perpendicular bisectors. One mislabelled point owns a whole wrong region.

决策区域是「离某训练点最近」的格子；边界是垂直平分线。一个错标点独占一整块错区。

---

## Practice / 练习

Points $(0,0)$ red, $(6,0)$ blue, $(3,4)$ red; query $(3,1)$. Euclidean 1-NN? If $(3,4)$ flips to blue?

<details><summary>Answer / 答案</summary>
Nearest is $(3,4)$ → red; if relabelled blue, prediction flips.
</details>
