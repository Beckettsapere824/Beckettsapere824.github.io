## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Imagine you are an intern in the ER. A new patient arrives with only a symptom vector (temperature, heart rate, blood pressure, …). In the cabinet are hundreds of past cases with the same kind of numbers and a final diagnosis (e.g. flu vs pneumonia).

想象你是急诊室的实习医生。来了一个新病人，手里只有一张症状表（体温、心率、血压……）。柜子里锁着过去几百个病例，每个都有同样的症状数字和最终诊断（比如「流感」或「肺炎」）。

You have no time for a textbook. The most naive idea is:

你没有时间翻教科书，最朴素的想法是：

> **Find the historically most similar case and copy its diagnosis.**
>
> **找历史上「症状最像」的那一个病人，看他得了什么病，就猜新病人也是这个病。**

That idea is **1-nearest neighbor (1-NN)**. Below we turn it into something we can compute, draw, and critique.

这就是 **1-最近邻（1-NN）**。下面把它变成可以计算、可以画图、可以讨论对错的东西。

---

## Step 1: What does “similar” mean? / 第一步：什么叫「像」？

Symptoms are lists of numbers—vectors. Similarity needs a **distance**: smaller means more similar.

症状是数字列表，也就是向量。「像不像」需要一个 **距离**：距离小 = 更像。

You already met three rulers in [M0.2](M0.2.md):

你在 [M0.2](M0.2.md) 已见过三种常用尺子：

| Name / 名称 | Formula (2D) / 公式 | Everyday picture / 生活类比 |
|-------------|---------------------|------------------------------|
| Euclidean $\ell_{2}$ | $\sqrt{(x_{1}-z_{1})^{2}+(x_{2}-z_{2})^{2}}$ | Crow-flies / 直线距离 |
| Manhattan $\ell_{1}$ | $\lvert x_{1}-z_{1}\rvert+\lvert x_{2}-z_{2}\rvert$ | Axis-aligned streets / 横平竖直 |
| Chebyshev $\ell_{\infty}$ | $\max(\lvert x_{1}-z_{1}\rvert,\lvert x_{2}-z_{2}\rvert)$ | Chess king / 棋盘国王一步 |

**Fact you will reuse:** changing the ruler can change who is nearest, and therefore the prediction. Distance is part of the model, not decoration.

**后面会反复用到的事实：** 换一把尺子，谁是「最近的病例」可能会变，预测也可能变。距离是模型的一部分，不是装饰。

---

## Step 2: Write the 1-NN rule / 第二步：把规则写清楚

Training data: labelled points $\{(\mathbf{x}_{i}, y_{i})\}_{i=1}^{n}$. A new point $\mathbf{x}$ arrives without a label.

训练数据：带标签的点 $\{(\mathbf{x}_{i}, y_{i})\}_{i=1}^{n}$。来了一个无标签的新点 $\mathbf{x}$。

1. Compute the distance from $\mathbf{x}$ to every training point.  
2. Let $i^{*}$ be the index of the closest one.  
3. Predict $\hat{y}(\mathbf{x}) = y_{i^{*}}$.

1. 算 $\mathbf{x}$ 到每个训练点的距离。  
2. 找到距离最小的下标 $i^{*}$。  
3. 预测 $\hat{y}(\mathbf{x}) = y_{i^{*}}$。

$$
\hat{y}(\mathbf{x}) = y_{i^{*}},\qquad
i^{*} = \arg\min_{i}\, d(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{x}_{i}).
$$

There is no training phase—you store the data. Prediction scans the library (or uses a search structure).

没有「训练阶段」——只是把数据存起来。预测时要扫全库（或借助搜索结构）。

---

## Step 3: What do decision regions look like? Draw a Voronoi diagram / 第三步：决策区域——手画 Voronoi

Formulas alone are empty. Use three training points and paint the plane by “who is nearest”.

只谈公式还是空的。用三个训练点，按「离谁最近」给平面涂色。

- $A=(0,0)$, label red / 红  
- $B=(4,0)$, label blue / 蓝  
- $C=(1,3)$, label red / 红

The boundaries are **perpendicular bisectors** between pairs of points. The partition is a **Voronoi diagram**: each training point owns the region closer to it than to any other point.

区域边界是点对之间的 **垂直平分线**。这样的划分叫 **Voronoi 图**：每个训练点占领「离它比离别人更近」的所有位置。

Do it once on paper:

请你自己做一次：

1. Bisector of $A$ and $B$ (the line $x=2$).  
2. Bisector of $A$ and $C$.  
3. Bisector of $B$ and $C$.  
4. Colour each cell with its owner’s label.

1. 画 $A$ 与 $B$ 的垂直平分线（$x=2$）。  
2. 画 $A$ 与 $C$ 的垂直平分线。  
3. 画 $B$ 与 $C$ 的垂直平分线。  
4. 每块涂上对应点的颜色。

Decision boundaries are piecewise straight and are **entirely determined by training locations**. Move one point and its cell deforms—that is why 1-NN is noise-sensitive: one wrong label owns a whole wrong region.

决策边界是分段直线，**完全由训练点位置决定**。挪动一个点，它的区域就变形——这就是 1-NN 对噪声敏感的几何原因：一个错标点独占一整块错误区域。

> Demo: [1-NN & Voronoi](https://beckettsapere824.github.io/ml-demos/knn-voronoi.html) — three points first, then add a noisy point and watch a region flip.
>
> 演示：先 3 个点，再加噪声点看区域被染错。

---

## Step 4: Change the ruler, change the boundary / 第四步：换尺子，边界就变

In the demo, switch Euclidean → Manhattan. Some labels flip; boundaries change shape.

在演示里把距离改成 Manhattan：有的位置颜色会变，边界形状也变。

So: **1-NN rule + a concrete distance = one classifier**. Saying “we use k-NN” without naming the distance leaves the model underspecified.

所以：**1-NN 规则 + 某一种距离 = 一个具体分类器**。只说「用 k-NN」却不说距离，模型并没有定义完整。

---

## Step 5: Character of 1-NN / 第五步：1-NN 的性格

| Strength / 优点 | Cost / 代价 |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Tiny implementation / 实现简单 | Prediction scans many points / 预测要查很多点 |
| Flexible boundary / 边界可以很曲折 | Sensitive to noise / 对噪声极度敏感 |
| No training phase / 无训练过程 | High dimension breaks “near” / 高维时「近」失效 |

---

## Summary / 小结

1. Classification = labelling regions of feature space. / 分类 = 在特征空间里给区域贴标签。  
2. 1-NN lets each training point own the region where it is nearest. / 1-NN 让每个训练点占领「离它最近」的区域。  
3. Ownership depends on the distance. / 占领方式由距离定义决定。  
4. The method memorizes data rather than compressing them into a short formula. / 记住数据，而不是压成短公式。

## Practice / 练习

Training points: $(0,0)$ red, $(6,0)$ blue, $(3,4)$ red. Query $Q=(3,1)$.

1. Euclidean 1-NN label? / 欧氏 1-NN？  
2. Manhattan? / 曼哈顿？  
3. If $(3,4)$ is mislabelled blue, does Euclidean prediction change? / $(3,4)$ 改蓝后预测变吗？

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

1. Nearest is $(3,4)$ → red.  
2. Still $(3,4)$ → red.  
3. Yes → blue. One noisy point decides the region.

</details>
