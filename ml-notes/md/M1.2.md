## The gap left by the previous node / 上一节留下的问题

In [M1.1](M1.1.md) each training point owns a whole Voronoi cell. If that point is mislabelled, the entire cell is wrong.

在 [M1.1](M1.1.md) 里每个训练点独占一整块区域。若该点标签是错的，整块都会错。

Back to the ER: trusting only the single most similar case is risky. A steadier idea:

回到急诊室：只信「最像的 1 个病例」太险。更稳的做法是：

> **Look at the $k$ most similar cases and let them vote.**
>
> **看最像的 $k$ 个病例，让他们投票。**

That is $k$-NN. We need: how to tally votes, how to break ties, whether nearer neighbours should speak louder, and what the knob $k$ does to the boundary.

这就是 $k$-NN。需要搞清：如何计票、平票怎么办、能否让更近的邻居更有话语权，以及 $k$ 对边界的影响。

---

## Step 1: Hard majority vote / 第一步：多数投票（硬投票）

1. Find the $k$ closest training points.  
2. Predict the label that appears most often among them.  
3. Prefer odd $k$ to reduce ties.

1. 找到最近的 $k$ 个训练点。  
2. 其中出现次数最多的标签作为预测。  
3. $k$ 常取奇数以减少平票。

With labels $y_{(1)},\ldots,y_{(k)}$ ordered by increasing distance:

将标签按距离从近到远记为 $y_{(1)},\ldots,y_{(k)}$：

$$
\hat{y}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathrm{mode}\{y_{(1)},\ldots,y_{(k)}\}.
$$

**Compute once before discussing intuition.**

**先手算一个，再谈感觉。**

| Neighbour / 邻居 | Distance / 距离 | Label / 标签 |
|------------------|-----------------|--------------|
| 1 | 0.1 | A |
| 2 | 0.2 | A |
| 3 | 0.3 | B |
| 4 | 0.4 | B |
| 5 | 0.5 | B |

For $k=5$: A appears twice, B three times → predict **B**.  
The two nearest are both A, but majority ignores nearness and only counts ballots.

$k=5$：A 两票，B 三票 → **B**。  
最近两个都是 A，但硬投票不在乎谁更近，只在乎票数。

---

## Step 2: Ties / 第二步：平票

For $k=4$ (first four rows): A,A,B,B → 2–2. The implementation needs a **fixed** tie-break, e.g. smaller total distance among tied classes, or lexicographic label order, or fall back to $k-1$.

$k=4$ 时 2–2 平票。必须预先固定破平规则，例如平票类中距离和更小者、标签序、或退回 $k-1$。

Without a deterministic rule, boundary cases are irreproducible.

没有确定性破平，边界情况无法复现。

---

## Step 3: Distance-weighted vote / 第三步：距离加权

Hard voting treats the neighbour at distance 0.1 the same as the one at 0.5. Better: weight by nearness

硬投票把距离 0.1 与 0.5 的邻居当成同等一票。改进：按近程度加权

$$
w_i = \frac{1}{d(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{x}_i)+\varepsilon},\qquad
\hat{y}(\mathbf{x}) = \arg\max_c \sum_{i \in N_k(\mathbf{x})} w_i \,\mathbf{1}_{y_i=c}.
$$

Same five neighbours:

同一组五个邻居：

- A: $1/0.1 + 1/0.2 = 15$  
- B: $1/0.3 + 1/0.4 + 1/0.5 \approx 7.83$

A wins. Hard vote said B; weighted vote says A. **Aggregation is part of the model.**

A 获胜。硬投票是 B，加权是 A。**聚合方式是模型的一部分。**

---

## Step 4: $k$ is a smoothing knob / 第四步：$k$ 是平滑旋钮

- **$k=1$**: boundary can twist tightly around every point—low bias, high variance (noise tears regions).  
- **$k$ near $n$**: almost global vote—smooth, high bias, low variance.  
- **Moderate $k$**: compromise; choose on a validation set.

- **$k=1$**：边界紧贴每个点——低偏差、高方差（噪声撕开区域）。  
- **$k$ 接近 $n$**：近乎全局投票——平滑、高偏差、低方差。  
- **中等 $k$**：折中；用验证集选。

Face example: five photos per person. $k=1$ lets one bad frame decide; $k=5$ pools all frames, stabler but duller boundary.

人脸：每人 5 张照片。$k=1$ 可能让单张坏图决定结果；$k=5$ 相当于全用上，更稳但边界更钝。

---

## Step 5: Relation to 1-NN / 第五步：和 1-NN 的关系

When $k=1$, both majority and weighted rules reduce to 1-NN. Extra choices are only: the value of $k$, hard vs weighted, and the distance from M1.1.

$k=1$ 时两种投票都退化成 1-NN。多出来的选择只有：$k$、硬/加权、以及 M1.1 的距离。

---

## Summary / 小结

| Question / 问题 | Answer / 答案 |
|-----------------|---------------|
| How to combine neighbours? | Majority or distance-weighted vote / 多数或距离加权 |
| Ties? | Fixed break rule / 固定破平规则 |
| Role of $k$? | Smoothing knob / 平滑旋钮 |
| Effect of weighting? | Nearer neighbours dominate; may flip the hard-vote result / 近邻主导，可能翻转硬投票结果 |

**Next:** in high dimension, “near” itself fails—the curse of dimensionality.

**下一问：** 高维里「近」会失效——维度灾难。
