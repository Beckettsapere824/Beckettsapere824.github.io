# M1.3 Curse of Dimensionality

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.2](M1.2.md)

---

In high dimension, nearest and farthest distances to a query become relatively close:

$$
\frac{d_{\max}-d_{\min}}{d_{\min}} \to 0.
$$

Then “nearest neighbour” loses contrast. Face pixels in $\mathbb{R}^{10000}$ are too sparse for raw k-NN; reduce dimension (PCA/LDA) first.

高维中最近与最远相对距离趋同，「最近邻」失去区分度。人脸原始像素对 k-NN 过疏，需先降维。
