## When “nearest” stops meaning much / 当「最近」不再可靠

The last two nodes assumed smaller distance ⇒ more similar, so nearest neighbours are sensible votes.

前两节默认：距离更小 ⇒ 更像，所以近邻投票合理。

In **high dimension** that geometric default collapses. Points become nearly equidistant from a query; the ranking of “who is closest” loses contrast, and k-NN approaches random guessing.

在 **高维** 中这一默认会崩掉：点到查询点的距离趋于接近，「谁更近」失去区分度，k-NN 接近瞎猜。

We will (1) see distance concentration in a thought experiment, (2) match it to face pixels, (3) motivate dimensionality reduction before k-NN (hook for M4).

本节：(1) 用思想实验看距离趋同；(2) 对人脸像素对号；(3) 引出先降维再分类（为 M4 埋钩）。

---

## Step 1: Points in a unit hypercube / 第一步：超立方体里的点

Sample many points uniformly in $[0,1]^d$ and a query $q$. Let $d_{\min}$ and $d_{\max}$ be the nearest and farthest distances to $q$. Watch

在 $[0,1]^d$ 中均匀撒点并随机查一个 $q$。记最近与最远距离为 $d_{\min}$、$d_{\max}$，观察

$$
\frac{d_{\max} - d_{\min}}{d_{\min}}.
$$

As $d$ grows, this ratio tends to **0**: the farthest and nearest points are relatively almost equally far.

维度升高时，该比值趋于 **0**：最远与最近相对来说几乎一样远。

```python
import numpy as np

def dist_ratio(d, n=2000):
    X = np.random.rand(n, d)
    q = np.random.rand(d)
    dists = np.linalg.norm(X - q, axis=1)
    return (dists.max() - dists.min()) / (dists.min() + 1e-12)

for d in [2, 10, 50, 200, 1000]:
    print(d, dist_ratio(d))
```

High-dimensional space is mostly empty; mass concentrates near the shell; pairwise distances concentrate. That is the **curse of dimensionality** for distance-based methods.

高维空间很空，质量挤在外壳附近，两两距离趋同。这就是距离方法下的 **维度灾难**。

---

## Step 2: Faces as vectors / 第二步：脸即向量

A $100\times 100$ grayscale face is a vector in $\mathbb{R}^{10000}$. Even thousands of training faces leave the space sparse—nearest neighbours are statistically unreliable.

$100\times 100$ 灰度脸是 $\mathbb{R}^{10000}$ 中的向量。即使有数千张训练脸，空间仍极稀疏——近邻在统计上不可靠。

So feeding raw pixels to k-NN usually fails: not because the voting idea is wrong, but because “near” is meaningless in that representation.

因此原始像素直接丢给 k-NN 通常很差：不是投票思想错了，而是这种表示下「近」没有意义。

---

## Step 3: Engineering way out / 第三步：工程出路

If faces vary mostly along a few directions (lighting, pose, expression), we can:

若脸主要沿少数方向变化（光照、姿态、表情），可以：

1. Find those directions (principal components—M4);  
2. Project each face to $50$–$100$ dimensions;  
3. Run k-NN in that denser space.

1. 找到这些方向（主成分——M4）；  
2. 投影到 $50$～$100$ 维；  
3. 在更「密」的空间里跑 k-NN。

Classic pipeline:

经典流水线：

$$
\mathrm{detect}\rightarrow\mathrm{align}\rightarrow\mathrm{vectorize}\rightarrow\textbf{PCA/LDA}\rightarrow k\text{-NN (or another classifier)}.
$$

Alignment matters: a few pixels of shift change the vector a lot; reduction cannot fix misalignment.

对齐很重要：平移几个像素向量就大变；不对齐则降维也救不回。

---

## Step 4: Clash with “more features help” / 第四步：与「多加特征更好」的冲突

Stacking features raises dimension and can worsen distance concentration if samples do not grow accordingly. For distance-based models, features must match sample size and geometry—not be piled blindly.

特征堆叠抬高维度，若样本量跟不上，距离趋同更严重。对基于距离的模型，特征须与样本量、几何匹配，不能盲目堆。

---

## Summary / 小结

| Phenomenon / 现象 | Consequence / 后果 | Way out / 出路 |
|-------------------|--------------------|----------------|
| $d_{\max}\approx d_{\min}$ in high $d$ | Neighbour ranks unstable / 近邻排序不稳 | Reduce dimension or change model / 降维或换模型 |
| Huge pixel dimension / 像素维数极高 | Raw k-NN weak / 原始 k-NN 弱 | PCA/LDA then classify / 先 PCA/LDA 再分类 |
| Blind feature growth / 盲目加特征 | May worsen the curse / 可能加重灾难 | Select, reduce, regularize / 选择、降维、正则 |

## Practice / 练习

1. Why does $(d_{\max}-d_{\min})/d_{\min}\to 0$ hurt k-NN? / 为何该比值趋于 0 会伤害 k-NN？  
2. With only 200 aligned faces, would you keep 10, 50, or 500 PCA dimensions—why? / 仅 200 张对齐脸时，PCA 保留 10、50 还是 500 维？为何？

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

1. All training points look roughly equally far; votes become unstable / near-random.  
2. 500 is still sparse for 200 points and keeps noise; 10 may drop identity signal; ~50 is a common starting range, tuned by validation.

1. 所有点几乎一样远，投票不稳。  
2. 500 对 200 样本仍空且含噪声；10 可能丢身份信息；约 50 维更常作起点，用验证集调。

</details>
