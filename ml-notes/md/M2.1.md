# M2.1 Linear Regression – Model & Squared Loss

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.8 Matrix calculus](M0.8.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

k-NN remembers every training point. Often we want a **short formula** that compresses the data: given features, spit out a number. The simplest such formula is a linear function of the features.

k-NN 记住每一个训练点。我们常常想要一个 **短公式** 来压缩数据：给定特征，吐出数值。最简单的公式是特征的线性函数。

---

## Step 1: A concrete scene / 第一步：具体情境

You have house size (㎡) and want to predict price. Points on a scatter plot almost lie along a straight line. Guessing a slope and intercept by eye is already linear regression—just not yet optimal.

已知房屋面积，预测房价。散点几乎沿一条直线。目测斜率与截距已经是线性回归——只是还没最优。

---

## Step 2: The model / 第二步：模型

With feature vector $\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$ (often including a constant $1$ for the intercept),

特征 $\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$（常含常数 $1$ 表示截距），

$$
\hat{y} = \mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}.
$$

Stack $n$ samples as rows of $X\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times d}$ and targets as $\mathbf{y}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}$. Then all predictions are $X\mathbf{w}$.

$n$ 个样本按行堆成 $X$，目标为 $\mathbf{y}$，全体预测为 $X\mathbf{w}$。

---

## Step 3: Why squared loss? / 第三步：为何平方损失？

Residual $\mathbf{r}=X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}$. We measure fit by

残差 $\mathbf{r}=X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}$。拟合用

$$
J(\mathbf{w})=\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}\rVert_{2}^{2}.
$$

Reasons: differentiable; large errors penalised more; coincides with Gaussian-noise MLE. Other losses exist; squared is the default entry point.

原因：可微；大误差惩罚更重；对应高斯噪声极大似然。还有别的损失；平方是入门默认。

---

## Step 4: What we optimise next / 第四步：下一步优化什么

Learning = choose $\mathbf{w}$ to make $J$ small. Two routes: set $\nabla J=\mathbf{0}$ (normal equations, M2.2) or walk downhill with gradient descent (M2.4).

学习 = 选 $\mathbf{w}$ 使 $J$ 小。两条路：令 $\nabla J=\mathbf{0}$（正规方程，M2.2）或梯度下降（M2.4）。

---

## Practice / 练习

For one feature $x$ and intercept, write $X$ for samples $(x_{i},y_{i})_{i=1}^{n}$.

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$X$ has rows $(1,x_{i})$; $\mathbf{w}=(w_{0},w_{1})^{\top}$; $\hat{y}_{i}=w_{0}+w_{1}x_{i}$.

$X$ 的行是 $(1,x_{i})$。

</details>
