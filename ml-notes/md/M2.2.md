# M2.2 Normal Equations & Closed-Form Solution

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.1 Linear regression](M2.1.md), [M0.4 Rank](M0.4.md), [M0.10 $\nabla f=0$](M0.10.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

We defined $J(\mathbf{w})=\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}\rVert_{2}^{2}$. Can we solve for the best $\mathbf{w}$ in closed form?

已有 $J$。能否一次写出最优 $\mathbf{w}$？

---

## Step 1: Set the gradient to zero / 第一步：令梯度为零

From [M0.8](M0.8.md),

$$
\nabla J = X^{\top}(X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}).
$$

Stationarity $\nabla J=\mathbf{0}$ becomes the **normal equations**

驻点条件即 **正规方程**

$$
X^{\top}X\,\mathbf{w}=X^{\top}\mathbf{y}.
$$

---

## Step 2: Geometry / 第二步：几何

$X\mathbf{w}$ lives in the column space of $X$. The normal equations say the residual $\mathbf{y}-X\mathbf{w}$ is orthogonal to every column of $X$—so $X\mathbf{w}$ is the orthogonal projection of $\mathbf{y}$ onto that column space.

$X\mathbf{w}$ 落在 $X$ 的列空间。正规方程说残差与每列正交——故 $X\mathbf{w}$ 是 $\mathbf{y}$ 到列空间的正交投影。

---

## Step 3: When is the solution unique? / 第三步：何时唯一？

$X^{\top}X$ invertible $\Leftrightarrow$ $X$ has full column rank (M0.4). Then

$$
\mathbf{w}^{*}=(X^{\top}X)^{-1}X^{\top}\mathbf{y}.
$$

If columns are dependent, infinitely many $\mathbf{w}$ achieve the same fit; use ridge (M0.8) or a pseudoinverse.

列相关时许多 $\mathbf{w}$ 拟合相同；用岭回归或伪逆。

---

## Step 4: Tiny example / 第四步：小例子

$X=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$, $\mathbf{y}=(1,2,3)^{\top}$.  
$X^{\top}X=\begin{pmatrix}3&3\\3&5\end{pmatrix}$, $X^{\top}\mathbf{y}=(6,8)^{\top}$.  
Solve: $\mathbf{w}^{*}=(1,1)^{\top}$. Predictions $(1,2,3)^{\top}$—perfect fit on this toy data.

解得 $\mathbf{w}^{*}=(1,1)^{\top}$，本例完美拟合。

---

## Practice / 练习

Why is $J$ guaranteed to have a global minimiser at any solution of the normal equations?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$J$ is convex (Hessian $X^{\top}X$ is PSD); any stationary point is global (M0.9–M0.10).

$J$ 凸，驻点即全局最小。

</details>
