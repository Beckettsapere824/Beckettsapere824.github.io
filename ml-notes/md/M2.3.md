# M2.3 Feature Maps & Polynomial Regression

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.2 Normal equations](M2.2.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

A straight line cannot fit a curved cloud. We keep the **linear-in-parameters** machinery of M2.1–M2.2, but feed it richer features.

直线拟合不了弯曲的点云。保持 M2.1–M2.2「对参数线性」的框架，只换更丰富的特征。

---

## Step 1: Feature map / 第一步：特征映射

Replace raw $\mathbf{x}$ by $\phi(\mathbf{x})$. The model is still

用 $\phi(\mathbf{x})$ 替换原始 $\mathbf{x}$。模型仍是

$$
\hat{y}=\mathbf{w}^{\top}\phi(\mathbf{x}),
$$

linear in $\mathbf{w}$, nonlinear in $\mathbf{x}$.

对 $\mathbf{w}$ 线性，对 $\mathbf{x}$ 可以非线性。

**Polynomial example (1D).** $\phi(x)=(1,x,x^{2},\ldots,x^{p})^{\top}$. Then $\hat{y}$ is a degree-$p$ polynomial.

一维多项式：$\phi(x)=(1,x,\ldots,x^{p})^{\top}$。

---

## Step 2: Same normal equations / 第二步：同一套正规方程

Build $\Phi\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times m}$ with rows $\phi(\mathbf{x}_{i})^{\top}$. Solve

$$
\Phi^{\top}\Phi\,\mathbf{w}=\Phi^{\top}\mathbf{y}.
$$

Nothing in the solver changes—only the design matrix.

求解器不变，变的只是设计矩阵。

---

## Step 3: Capacity vs overfitting / 第三步：容量与过拟合

Higher $p$ fits training data more tightly and can oscillate between points (Runge-like behaviour). Prefer validation error over training error when choosing $p$; regularisation (ridge) stabilises large $p$.

$p$ 越大训练集贴合越紧，点间可能振荡。用验证误差选 $p$；岭回归稳定高阶。

---

## Practice / 练习

For $x\in\mathbb{R}$ and $p=2$, write $\phi(x)$ and the prediction formula.

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$\phi(x)=(1,x,x^{2})^{\top}$, $\hat{y}=w_{0}+w_{1}x+w_{2}x^{2}$.

</details>
