# M2.4 Gradient Descent for Linear Models

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.1](M2.1.md), [M0.8](M0.8.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Normal equations need $X^{\top}X$ (or a linear solve of size $d$). When $d$ is huge or the loss is not quadratic, we minimise by **walking downhill**.

正规方程要解 $d$ 维线性系。$d$ 很大或损失非二次时，改为 **沿坡下山**。

---

## Step 1: The update / 第一步：更新式

$$
\mathbf{w}\leftarrow\mathbf{w}-\eta\,\nabla J(\mathbf{w}).
$$

$\eta>0$ is the **learning rate**. For squared loss, $\nabla J=X^{\top}(X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y})$ (or a mini-batch version).

$\eta$ 是 **学习率**。平方损失下梯度为 $X^{\top}(X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y})$（或小批量近似）。

---

## Step 2: Full batch vs SGD / 第二步：全批量与 SGD

| Mode | Gradient uses | Behaviour |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Batch | all $n$ rows | stable, expensive per step |
| SGD | one row | noisy, cheap, can escape shallow traps |
| Mini-batch | $B$ rows | practical default |

全批量稳但贵；单样本噪声大；小批量是实务默认。

---

## Step 3: Learning rate intuition / 第三步：学习率直觉

Too large $\eta$: $J$ diverges. Too small: crawls. For convex smooth $J$, sufficiently small $\eta$ converges to a global minimiser; choosing $\eta$ is still empirical (line search, schedules).

$\eta$ 过大发散，过小爬行。凸且光滑时足够小的 $\eta$ 收敛到全局最小；具体取值仍靠实验。

---

## Step 4: Link to normal equations / 第四步：与正规方程的关系

At convergence of GD on $J$, $\nabla J\approx\mathbf{0}$, i.e. the same stationarity condition as the normal equations. GD is an *algorithm* to reach that point; the normal equation is the *algebraic statement* of that point.

GD 收敛时 $\nabla J\approx\mathbf{0}$，与正规方程同一驻点条件。GD 是到达该点的算法，正规方程是该点的代数表述。

---

## Practice / 练习

Write one GD step for $J(\mathbf{w})=\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}\rVert_{2}^{2}$.

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$\mathbf{w}\leftarrow\mathbf{w}-\eta X^{\top}(X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y})$.

</details>
