# M2.6 Adaline & Continuous Activation

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.5](M2.5.md), [M2.1](M2.1.md)

---

Continuous score + squared loss = least squares:

$$
J=\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}\rVert_{2}^{2},
\quad
\mathbf{w}\leftarrow\mathbf{w}-\eta X^{\top}(X\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{y}).
$$

Updates every step (not only errors). Converges without separability.

每步都更新；不要求可分也能收敛。
