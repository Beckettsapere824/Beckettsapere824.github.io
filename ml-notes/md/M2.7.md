# M2.7 Logistic Regression – Model & Cross-Entropy Loss

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.6 Adaline](M2.6.md), [M0.8 Matrix calculus](M0.8.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Adaline’s score is unbounded. We often want a **probability** that the label is positive, and a loss that heavily punishes confident mistakes.

Adaline 的分数无界。我们常需要「为正类的 **概率**」，以及严惩「很自信的错误」的损失。

---

## Step 1: Sigmoid / 第一步：Sigmoid

$$
\sigma(z)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-z}}\in(0,1),\qquad
p(y=1\mid\mathbf{x})=\sigma(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}).
$$

---

## Step 2: Likelihood → cross-entropy / 第二步：似然 → 交叉熵

For labels $y_{i}\in\{0,1\}$, maximising likelihood is equivalent to minimising

标签 $y_{i}\in\{0,1\}$ 时，极大似然等价于最小化

$$
J(\mathbf{w})=-\sum_{i}\bigl[y_{i}\log\sigma(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i})+(1-y_{i})\log\bigl(1-\sigma(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i})\bigr)\bigr].
$$

This is **binary cross-entropy**. A confident wrong prediction ($\sigma\to 0$ while $y=1$) drives $-\log\sigma\to+\infty$.

这是 **二元交叉熵**。很自信却错了会让损失趋向无穷。

---

## Step 3: Gradient has a familiar shape / 第三步：梯度形式眼熟

With $\mathbf{p}=\sigma(X\mathbf{w})$ elementwise,

$$
\nabla J = X^{\top}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{y}).
$$

Same pattern as least squares with residual $\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{y}$. $J$ is convex in $\mathbf{w}$, so GD reaches a global minimiser.

与最小二乘同形（残差换成 $\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{y}$）。$J$ 对 $\mathbf{w}$ 凸，GD 达全局最小。

---

## Practice / 练习

If $\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}=0$, what is $\sigma(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x})$? What does that probability mean?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$\sigma(0)=1/2$: the model is maximally uncertain between the two classes.

$\sigma(0)=1/2$：两类之间最不确定。

</details>
