# M2.8 Multiclass Softmax & Cross-Entropy

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.7 Logistic regression](M2.7.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Binary logistic handles two classes. For $K$ classes we need $K$ scores that become a probability vector summing to one.

二元逻辑回归处理两类。$K$ 类需要 $K$ 个分数变成和为 1 的概率向量。

---

## Step 1: One linear score per class / 第一步：每类一个线性分数

Weight matrix $W\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times K}$. Scores $\mathbf{z}=W^{\top}\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{K}$.

权重矩阵 $W\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times K}$。分数 $\mathbf{z}=W^{\top}\mathbf{x}$。

---

## Step 2: Softmax / 第二步：Softmax

$$
p_{k}=\frac{e^{z_{k}}}{\sum_{j=1}^{K}e^{z_{j}}}.
$$

**Numerics:** replace $z_{k}$ by $z_{k}-\max_{j}z_{j}$ before exponentiating to avoid overflow.

**数值：** 先减 $\max_{j}z_{j}$ 再指数，避免溢出。

---

## Step 3: Cross-entropy gradient / 第三步：交叉熵梯度

If the true class is $y$, loss $-\log p_{y}$. Letting $\mathbf{p}$ be the softmax vector and $\mathbf{e}_{y}$ the one-hot label,

真类为 $y$ 时损失 $-\log p_{y}$。记 $\mathbf{p}$ 为 softmax 向量、$\mathbf{e}_{y}$ 为 one-hot，

$$
\frac{\partial J}{\partial\mathbf{z}}=\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{e}_{y}.
$$

Clean and standard: this is the usual last layer of a neural net classifier.

干净且标准：神经网络分类器最后一层通常就是这样。

---

## Step 4: Link forward / 第四步：向后衔接

Stack many linear maps with nonlinearities between them and you have a deep net; backprop (M8) is the chain rule for that stack. Softmax + cross-entropy remains the default head.

多层线性与中间非线性叠起来就是深度网络；反向传播（M8）是链式法则。Softmax + 交叉熵仍是默认头。

---

## Practice / 练习

Scores $\mathbf{z}=(2,2,2)^{\top}$. What is $\mathbf{p}$?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Uniform: $\mathbf{p}=(1/3,1/3,1/3)^{\top}$.

均匀：$\mathbf{p}=(1/3,1/3,1/3)^{\top}$。

</details>
