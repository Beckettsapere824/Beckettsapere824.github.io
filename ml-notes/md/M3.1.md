# M3.1 Maximum-Margin Hyperplane (Hard Margin)

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.1 Vectors](M0.1.md), [M0.6 PSD](M0.6.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Many hyperplanes can separate two linearly separable classes. Which one is safest when a test point is slightly noisy? The one that leaves the **widest empty strip** between the classes.

两类线性可分时，能分开的超平面有很多。测试点略有噪声时哪条最安全？在两类之间留下 **最宽空白带** 的那条。

---

## Step 1: Function margin vs geometric margin / 第一步：函数间隔与几何间隔

A classifier $\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}+b$ assigns label $\mathrm{sign}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}+b)$. Scaling $(\mathbf{w},b)$ by a positive constant does not change the decision boundary, but multiplies the raw scores. The **geometric margin** of a correctly classified point is the Euclidean distance to the plane:

分类器 $\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}+b$ 用符号作标签。正数缩放 $(\mathbf{w},b)$ 不改变分界面，但会放大分数。正确分类点的 **几何间隔** 是到平面的欧氏距离：

$$
\gamma_{i}=\frac{y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)}{\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}}.
$$

The geometric margin of a hyperplane is $\min_{i}\gamma_{i}$. We want to maximise that minimum.

超平面的几何间隔是 $\min_{i}\gamma_{i}$。目标是最大化这个最小值。

---

## Step 2: Canonical form / 第二步：规范形式

Fix the **functional** margin of the closest points to be $1$:

把最近点的 **函数** 间隔固定为 $1$：

$$
y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)\ge 1\quad\forall i.
$$

Then maximising geometric margin is equivalent to minimising $\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$ (or $\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}$ for differentiability).

此时最大化几何间隔 $\Leftrightarrow$ 最小化 $\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$（为可微常写 $\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}$）。

---

## Step 3: Hard-margin primal / 第三步：硬间隔原问题

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min_{\mathbf{w},b}\quad & \tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2} \\
\mathrm{s.t.}\quad & y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)\ge 1,\quad i=1,\ldots,n.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is a convex quadratic programme. **Support vectors** are points that meet the constraint with equality—they lie on the margin boundaries and alone determine the solution.

这是凸二次规划。**支持向量** 是使约束取等号的点——贴在间隔边界上，单独决定解。

---

## Step 4: Picture / 第四步：图景

Two parallel planes $\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}+b=\pm 1$ sandwich an empty slab of width $2/\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$. Wider slab $\Leftrightarrow$ smaller $\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$.

两平行平面 $\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}+b=\pm 1$ 夹出宽度 $2/\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$ 的空带。带宽 $\Leftrightarrow$ $\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$ 小。

Interactive geometry: [SVM margin demo](https://beckettsapere824.github.io/ml-demos/svm-margin.html).

---

## Practice / 练习

Why is maximising $2/\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$ under functional margin $\ge 1$ the same as minimising $\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}$?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$2/\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$ increases exactly when $\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$ decreases; squaring and the factor $\tfrac{1}{2}$ are monotone reparameterisations that make the objective smooth and QP-friendly.

$2/\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$ 随 $\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}$ 减小而增大；平方与 $\tfrac{1}{2}$ 是便于 QP 的光滑重参数化。

</details>
