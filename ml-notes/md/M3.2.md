# M3.2 Hard-Margin Dual Derivation

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Hard margin](M3.1.md), [M0.11 Lagrange](M0.11.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

The primal is constrained in $(\mathbf{w},b)$. The dual will show two facts the course leans on repeatedly: $\mathbf{w}$ is a combination of training points, and the objective depends on data only through **inner products**.

原问题在 $(\mathbf{w},b)$ 上有约束。对偶将反复用到的两点：$\mathbf{w}$ 是训练点的组合，目标对数据的依赖只通过 **内积**。

---

## Step 1: Lagrangian / 第一步：Lagrangian

Multipliers $\alpha_{i}\ge 0$ for $y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)\ge 1$:

对 $y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)\ge 1$ 引入 $\alpha_{i}\ge 0$：

$$
L(\mathbf{w},b,\boldsymbol{\alpha})=\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}-\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}\bigl[y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)-1\bigr].
$$

---

## Step 2: Stationarity in $\mathbf{w},b$ / 第二步：对 $\mathbf{w},b$ 驻点

$$
\nabla_{\mathbf{w}}L=\mathbf{w}-\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}\mathbf{x}_{i}=\mathbf{0}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\mathbf{w}=\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}\mathbf{x}_{i}.
$$

$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial b}=-\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}=0.
$$

So the weight vector lives in the span of the training samples.

因此权重落在训练样本张成的空间里。

---

## Step 3: Dual objective / 第三步：对偶目标

Substitute $\mathbf{w}$ back into $L$ and use $\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}=0$:

代回 $L$ 并利用 $\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}=0$：

$$
\max_{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}-\tfrac{1}{2}\sum_{i,j}\alpha_{i}\alpha_{j}y_{i}y_{j}\,\mathbf{x}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{j}
\quad\mathrm{s.t.}\quad
\alpha_{i}\ge 0,\;\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}=0.
$$

Only inner products $\mathbf{x}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{j}$ appear—ready for kernels (M3.4).

只出现内积 $\mathbf{x}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{j}$——为核（M3.4）做好准备。

---

## Step 4: Support vectors via KKT / 第四步：KKT 与支持向量

Complementary slackness: $\alpha_{i}\bigl[y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)-1\bigr]=0$.  
Thus $\alpha_{i}>0$ forces the point to lie on the margin—those are the **support vectors**. All other points can be removed without changing $\mathbf{w}$.

互补松弛：$\alpha_{i}>0$ 的点必须贴在间隔上，即 **支持向量**；其余点去掉不改变 $\mathbf{w}$。

---

## Practice / 练习

If $\alpha_{3}=0$, can point $3$ be deleted from the training set without changing the hard-margin solution?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Yes—it is not a support vector and does not enter $\mathbf{w}=\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}\mathbf{x}_{i}$.

可以——不是支持向量，不进入 $\mathbf{w}$ 的和式。

</details>
