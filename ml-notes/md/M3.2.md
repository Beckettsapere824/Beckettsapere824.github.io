# M3.2 Hard-Margin Dual

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1](M3.1.md), [M0.11](M0.11.md)

---

$$
\mathbf{w}=\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}\mathbf{x}_{i},
\quad
\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}=0.
$$

Dual depends on data only via $\mathbf{x}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{j}$. $\alpha_{i}>0$ $\Rightarrow$ support vector.

对偶只通过内积依赖数据；$\alpha_{i}>0$ 为支持向量。
