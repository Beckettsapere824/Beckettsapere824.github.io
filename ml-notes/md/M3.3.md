# M3.3 Soft-Margin SVM & Slack Variables

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.2 Dual](M3.2.md), [M0.12 KKT](M0.12.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Real data almost always overlap. Hard margin then has **no feasible point**. Soft margin allows a few violations while still preferring a wide strip.

真实数据几乎总有重叠。硬间隔会 **不可行**。软间隔允许少量越界，仍尽量保持宽带。

---

## Step 1: Slack variables / 第一步：松弛变量

For each $i$ introduce $\xi_{i}\ge 0$ and relax

对每个 $i$ 引入 $\xi_{i}\ge 0$，放宽为

$$
y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)\ge 1-\xi_{i}.
$$

$\xi_{i}=0$: outside or on the margin (correct side). $0<\xi_{i}\le 1$: inside the margin strip. $\xi_{i}>1$: misclassified.

$\xi_{i}=0$：间隔外或边界上；$0<\xi_{i}\le 1$：落入间隔带；$\xi_{i}>1$：分错。

---

## Step 2: Soft-margin primal / 第二步：软间隔原问题

$$
\min_{\mathbf{w},b,\boldsymbol{\xi}}\;\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}+C\sum_{i}\xi_{i}
\quad\mathrm{s.t.}\quad
y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)\ge 1-\xi_{i},\;\xi_{i}\ge 0.
$$

**$C$** trades off margin width against total slack. Large $C$: few violations allowed (closer to hard margin). Small $C$: smoother, more tolerance.

**$C$** 权衡间隔宽度与总松弛。$C$ 大：少容忍错误；$C$ 小：更平滑、更容忍。

---

## Step 3: Dual box constraints / 第三步：对偶盒约束

The dual looks like the hard-margin dual, except

对偶形式类似硬间隔，但

$$
0\le\alpha_{i}\le C.
$$

Upper bound $C$ is the KKT shadow of the slack penalties (M0.12). Points with $0<\alpha_{i}<C$ still sit on the margin; $\alpha_{i}=C$ are on or beyond the wrong side of the margin boundary.

上界 $C$ 是松弛惩罚的 KKT 影子。$0<\alpha_{i}<C$ 仍贴间隔；$\alpha_{i}=C$ 在间隔内侧或错侧。

---

## Step 4: Hinge loss view / 第四步：铰链损失视角

Eliminating $\xi$ shows the problem is equivalent to

消去 $\xi$ 后等价于

$$
\min_{\mathbf{w},b}\;\tfrac{1}{2}\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}+C\sum_{i}\max\bigl(0,1-y_{i}(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i}+b)\bigr).
$$

Regularised empirical hinge loss—same spirit as logistic regression, different loss.

正则化经验铰链损失——与逻辑回归同精神、不同损失。

---

## Practice / 练习

What happens conceptually as $C\to\infty$ on a linearly separable set?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Violations become infinitely expensive, so the optimum recovers the hard-margin SVM.

越界代价趋于无穷，最优解回到硬间隔 SVM。

</details>
