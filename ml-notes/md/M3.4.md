# M3.4 Kernel Trick & Positive-Definite Kernels

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.2 Dual](M3.2.md), [M0.6 PSD](M0.6.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Some labels are not linearly separable in the original coordinates. Mapping $\mathbf{x}\mapsto\phi(\mathbf{x})$ into a richer space can fix that—but $\phi$ may be huge or infinite-dimensional. The dual only needs **inner products** of the $\phi(\mathbf{x}_{i})$.

原坐标下可能线性不可分。映射到更丰富的 $\phi(\mathbf{x})$ 可以分开——但 $\phi$ 可能极高维甚至无限维。对偶只需要 $\phi(\mathbf{x}_{i})$ 的 **内积**。

---

## Step 1: Replace inner products / 第一步：替换内积

Hard/soft duals depend on data through $\mathbf{x}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{j}$. Define a **kernel**

硬/软对偶通过 $\mathbf{x}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{j}$ 依赖数据。定义 **核**

$$
k(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{z})=\phi(\mathbf{x})^{\top}\phi(\mathbf{z}).
$$

If $k$ is cheap to evaluate, never form $\phi$ explicitly. Swap every $\mathbf{x}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{j}$ for $k(\mathbf{x}_{i},\mathbf{x}_{j})$.

若 $k$ 便宜，就不必显式构造 $\phi$。把每个 $\mathbf{x}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{j}$ 换成 $k(\mathbf{x}_{i},\mathbf{x}_{j})$。

---

## Step 2: Valid kernels (Mercer) / 第二步：合法核（Mercer）

For any finite set of points, the Gram matrix $K_{ij}=k(\mathbf{x}_{i},\mathbf{x}_{j})$ must be **PSD**. Otherwise the dual is no longer a concave maximisation and the geometry breaks.

任意有限点集上 Gram 矩阵 $K_{ij}=k(\mathbf{x}_{i},\mathbf{x}_{j})$ 必须 **半正定**。否则对偶不再是凹最大化，几何也会坏掉。

---

## Step 3: Common kernels / 第三步：常用核

| Kernel | Formula | Feature view |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| Linear | $\mathbf{x}^{\top}\mathbf{z}$ | $\phi=\mathrm{id}$ |
| Polynomial | $(\mathbf{x}^{\top}\mathbf{z}+c)^{p}$ | finite monomials |
| RBF / Gaussian | $\exp(-\gamma\lVert\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{z}\rVert_{2}^{2})$ | infinite-dimensional |

RBF is the default “flexible separator” when you do not know a good finite feature map.

不知道好的有限特征时，RBF 是默认的灵活分隔器。

---

## Step 4: Decision still a sum over support vectors / 第四步：决策仍是支持向量求和

$$
f(\mathbf{x})=\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}y_{i}k(\mathbf{x}_{i},\mathbf{x})+b.
$$

Prediction cost scales with the number of support vectors, not the ambient dimension of $\phi$.

预测代价随支持向量个数增长，而不是 $\phi$ 的环境维数。

---

## Practice / 练习

Why is an arbitrary similarity $s(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{z})$ not necessarily a valid kernel?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Its Gram matrix might have a negative eigenvalue, so there is no feature map $\phi$ with $s=\phi^{\top}\phi$, and the SVM dual may be unbounded or meaningless.

Gram 矩阵可能有负特征值，不存在 $\phi$ 使 $s=\phi^{\top}\phi$，对偶可能无界或无意义。

</details>
