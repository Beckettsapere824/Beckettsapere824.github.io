# M3.4 Kernel Trick

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.2](M3.2.md), [M0.6](M0.6.md)

---

$$
k(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{z})=\phi(\mathbf{x})^{\top}\phi(\mathbf{z}).
$$

Replace $\mathbf{x}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{j}$ by $k(\mathbf{x}_{i},\mathbf{x}_{j})$. Gram matrix must be PSD (Mercer). RBF: $\exp(-\gamma\lVert\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{z}\rVert_{2}^{2})$.

用核替换内积；Gram 须 PSD。RBF 如上。
