# M3.5 Representer Theorem Intuition & Kernel Decision Function

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.4 Kernel trick](M3.4.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

If $\phi$ is infinite-dimensional, is the optimal $\mathbf{w}$ infinite-dimensional too? The representer theorem says the optimum still lies in the **finite-dimensional** span of the training mapped points.

若 $\phi$ 无限维，最优 $\mathbf{w}$ 是否也无限维？表示定理说：最优解仍落在训练映射点张成的 **有限维** 子空间里。

---

## Step 1: Orthogonal split / 第一步：正交分解

Write any candidate weight as

任意候选权重写成

$$
\mathbf{w}=\mathbf{w}_{\parallel}+\mathbf{w}_{\perp},
$$

where $\mathbf{w}_{\parallel}$ is in $\mathrm{span}\{\phi(\mathbf{x}_{1}),\ldots,\phi(\mathbf{x}_{n})\}$ and $\mathbf{w}_{\perp}$ is orthogonal to that span.

其中 $\mathbf{w}_{\parallel}$ 在 $\mathrm{span}\{\phi(\mathbf{x}_{1}),\ldots,\phi(\mathbf{x}_{n})\}$ 中，$\mathbf{w}_{\perp}$ 与之正交。

---

## Step 2: Regulariser punishes the orthogonal part / 第二步：正则项惩罚正交部分

Data terms depend on $\mathbf{w}$ only through inner products $\mathbf{w}^{\top}\phi(\mathbf{x}_{i})=\mathbf{w}_{\parallel}^{\top}\phi(\mathbf{x}_{i})$. The penalty $\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}=\lVert\mathbf{w}_{\parallel}\rVert_{2}^{2}+\lVert\mathbf{w}_{\perp}\rVert_{2}^{2}$ is strictly larger whenever $\mathbf{w}_{\perp}\neq\mathbf{0}$. Hence an optimum may take $\mathbf{w}_{\perp}=\mathbf{0}$.

数据项只通过 $\mathbf{w}^{\top}\phi(\mathbf{x}_{i})=\mathbf{w}_{\parallel}^{\top}\phi(\mathbf{x}_{i})$ 依赖 $\mathbf{w}$。惩罚 $\lVert\mathbf{w}\rVert_{2}^{2}=\lVert\mathbf{w}_{\parallel}\rVert_{2}^{2}+\lVert\mathbf{w}_{\perp}\rVert_{2}^{2}$ 在 $\mathbf{w}_{\perp}\neq\mathbf{0}$ 时严格更大。故最优可取 $\mathbf{w}_{\perp}=\mathbf{0}$。

---

## Step 3: Finite expansion / 第三步：有限展开

$$
\mathbf{w}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}y_{i}\phi(\mathbf{x}_{i})
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
f(\mathbf{x})=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_{i}y_{i}k(\mathbf{x}_{i},\mathbf{x})+b.
$$

Parameter count tracks $n$ (or the number of support vectors), not $\dim\phi$.

参数量跟 $n$（或支持向量数）走，而不是 $\dim\phi$。

---

## Step 4: Course takeaway / 第四步：本课收束

SVM + kernel = convex optimisation + geometry of margins + computations that never leave the original space except through $k$. Next major branch is unsupervised geometry (PCA, clustering).

SVM + 核 = 凸优化 + 间隔几何 + 除 $k$ 外不离开原空间的计算。下一条主线是无监督几何（PCA、聚类）。

---

## Practice / 练习

Does a valid kernel method’s number of free parameters grow with the feature-space dimension?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

No—after the representer reduction it grows with the number of training points (sparsified by support vectors).

不——表示定理之后随训练点数增长（再由支持向量稀疏化）。

</details>
