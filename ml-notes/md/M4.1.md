# M4.1 Covariance Matrix & Empirical Second-Moment

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3 Matrices](M0.3.md), [M0.6 PSD](M0.6.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

High-dimensional data sit in a coordinate system that is often arbitrary. We want a matrix that describes **how the cloud stretches** in every direction—so we can later find the longest axes (PCA).

高维数据的坐标轴往往是任意的。我们需要一个矩阵描述点云在各个方向上 **如何伸展**，以便找到最长的轴（PCA）。

---

## Step 1: Centre the data / 第一步：中心化

Let rows of $X\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times d}$ be samples. Subtract the mean $\bar{\mathbf{x}}=\tfrac{1}{n}\sum_{i}\mathbf{x}_{i}$ from every row to get $\tilde{X}$. Shape, not location, is what covariance records.

$X$ 的行是样本。每行减去均值 $\bar{\mathbf{x}}$ 得 $\tilde{X}$。协方差记录的是形状，不是位置。

---

## Step 2: Covariance matrix / 第二步：协方差矩阵

$$
\Sigma = \frac{1}{n}\tilde{X}^{\top}\tilde{X}\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d}.
$$

(Some texts use $1/(n-1)$; for PCA the scale only rescales eigenvalues uniformly.) Entry $(j,k)$ is the empirical covariance between features $j$ and $k$. $\Sigma$ is always **symmetric PSD**.

（有的书用 $1/(n-1)$；对 PCA 只是整体缩放特征值。）$(j,k)$ 元是特征 $j$ 与 $k$ 的经验协方差。$\Sigma$ 总是 **对称半正定**。

---

## Step 3: Variance in a direction / 第三步：某方向上的方差

For a unit vector $\mathbf{u}$,

对单位向量 $\mathbf{u}$，

$$
\mathbf{u}^{\top}\Sigma\mathbf{u}
= \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i}(\tilde{\mathbf{x}}_{i}^{\top}\mathbf{u})^{2}
$$

is the empirical variance of the scalar projections onto $\mathbf{u}$. Large value = cloud stretches far along that direction.

是投影到 $\mathbf{u}$ 上的经验方差。大 = 点云沿该方向拉得很长。

---

## Practice / 练习

Why must we centre before interpreting $\mathbf{u}^{\top}(X^{\top}X)\mathbf{u}$ as variance?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Without centering, the quadratic form mixes mean location with spread; a far-away mean inflates the value even if the cloud is tiny.

不中心化会把均值位置混进散布；均值很远时即使点云很小，二次型也会很大。

</details>
