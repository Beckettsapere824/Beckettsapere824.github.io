# M4.2 PCA – Principal Axes via Eigendecomposition

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.1 Covariance](M4.1.md), [M0.5 Eigen](M0.5.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

We can measure variance along any direction via $\mathbf{u}^{\top}\Sigma\mathbf{u}$. Which unit direction maximises it? That direction is the first principal axis.

可用 $\mathbf{u}^{\top}\Sigma\mathbf{u}$ 量任意方向的方差。哪个单位方向最大？那就是第一主轴。

---

## Step 1: Rayleigh quotient / 第一步：Rayleigh 商

$$
\max_{\lVert\mathbf{u}\rVert_{2}=1}\mathbf{u}^{\top}\Sigma\mathbf{u}.
$$

By the spectral theorem for symmetric $\Sigma$, the maximum is the largest eigenvalue $\lambda_{1}$, attained at a corresponding unit eigenvector $\mathbf{u}_{1}$ (M0.5).

由对称矩阵谱定理，最大值是最大特征值 $\lambda_{1}$，在单位特征向量 $\mathbf{u}_{1}$ 处取得。

---

## Step 2: Further axes / 第二步：后续主轴

The second principal axis maximises variance among directions orthogonal to $\mathbf{u}_{1}$; it is the eigenvector for the second-largest eigenvalue. Continuing gives an orthonormal basis $\mathbf{u}_{1},\ldots,\mathbf{u}_{d}$ with $\Sigma=U\Lambda U^{\top}$.

第二主轴在与 $\mathbf{u}_{1}$ 正交的方向中再最大化方差，即次大特征值的特征向量。继续下去得到标准正交基与谱分解 $\Sigma=U\Lambda U^{\top}$。

---

## Step 3: Truncation error / 第三步：截断误差

Projecting onto the first $k$ axes and reconstructing discards variance equal to $\sum_{j=k+1}^{d}\lambda_{j}$. Small trailing eigenvalues ≈ safe to drop for compression.

投影到前 $k$ 个轴再重构，丢掉的方差是 $\sum_{j=k+1}^{d}\lambda_{j}$。尾部小特征值 ≈ 可安全丢掉以压缩。

---

## Practice / 练习

If $\Sigma$ has eigenvalues $5,1,0.1$, what fraction of variance do the first two components keep?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$(5+1)/(5+1+0.1)=6/6.1\approx 98.4\%$.

</details>
