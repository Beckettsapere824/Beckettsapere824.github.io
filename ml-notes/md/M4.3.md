# M4.3 PCA – Algorithm, Whitening, Eigenfaces

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.2 PCA eigen](M4.2.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Theory says “eigen-decompose $\Sigma$.” Practice asks for a checklist, a numerical alternative (SVD), and a classic picture: **eigenfaces**.

理论说「对 $\Sigma$ 做特征分解」。实务需要清单、数值替代（SVD）和经典图像：**特征脸**。

---

## Step 1: Pipeline / 第一步：流水线

1. Centre columns of $X$ → $\tilde{X}$.  
2. Compute $\Sigma=\tfrac{1}{n}\tilde{X}^{\top}\tilde{X}$ **or** thin SVD $\tilde{X}=U\Sigma_{\mathrm{svd}}V^{\top}$ (right singular vectors = principal axes).  
3. Keep top $k$ axes as columns of $W\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times k}$.  
4. Codes: $Z=\tilde{X}W$. Reconstruct: $\hat{X}=ZW^{\top}+\mathbf{1}\bar{\mathbf{x}}^{\top}$.

1. 中心化。2. 特征分解或 SVD。3. 取前 $k$ 轴。4. 投影与重构。

---

## Step 2: Whitening / 第二步：白化

After projection, divide coordinate $j$ by $\sqrt{\lambda_{j}}$ (or soft-threshold small $\lambda$). Whitened features have identity covariance—useful before some distance-based methods.

投影后第 $j$ 坐标除以 $\sqrt{\lambda_{j}}$。白化后协方差为单位阵，便于某些基于距离的方法。

---

## Step 3: Eigenfaces / 第三步：特征脸

Each principal axis is a vector in pixel space. Reshape it to an image: those are **eigenfaces**—data-driven basis images showing dominant appearance variations (lighting, pose trends, etc.), not hand-designed features. Ties back to the face pipeline in [M1.3](M1.3.md).

每个主轴是像素空间中的向量，还原成图像即 **特征脸**——数据驱动的基底，显示主要外观变化，不是人工特征。与 [M1.3](M1.3.md) 人脸流水线闭环。

---

## Practice / 练习

Why can $k$ that is too large hurt downstream classifiers?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Trailing components often encode noise; keeping them re-introduces the curse of dimensionality you tried to escape.

尾部成分常编码噪声；保留它们等于把维度灾难请回来。

</details>
