# M4.4 LDA – Fisher Criterion

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.1 Covariance](M4.1.md), [M0.5 Eigen](M0.5.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

PCA keeps variance whether or not it helps **separate labels**. With labels, we want directions where classes sit far apart and each class is tight.

PCA 保方差，不管是否有助于 **分开标签**。有标签时，我们要类间远、类内紧的方向。

---

## Step 1: Scatter matrices / 第一步：散度矩阵

For classes $c=1,\ldots,C$ with means $\boldsymbol{\mu}_{c}$ and global mean $\boldsymbol{\mu}$:

对各类均值 $\boldsymbol{\mu}_{c}$ 与总均值 $\boldsymbol{\mu}$：

- **Within-class** $S_{W}=\sum_{c}\sum_{i\in c}(\mathbf{x}_{i}-\boldsymbol{\mu}_{c})(\mathbf{x}_{i}-\boldsymbol{\mu}_{c})^{\top}$  
- **Between-class** $S_{B}=\sum_{c}n_{c}(\boldsymbol{\mu}_{c}-\boldsymbol{\mu})(\boldsymbol{\mu}_{c}-\boldsymbol{\mu})^{\top}$

---

## Step 2: Fisher criterion / 第二步：Fisher 准则

$$
J(\mathbf{u})=\frac{\mathbf{u}^{\top}S_{B}\mathbf{u}}{\mathbf{u}^{\top}S_{W}\mathbf{u}}.
$$

Numerator: variance of projected class means (want large). Denominator: average within-class variance after projection (want small).

分子：投影后类均值的分散（越大越好）。分母：投影后类内方差（越小越好）。

---

## Step 3: LDA is a projection / 第三步：LDA 是投影

LDA here means finding good linear features for separation—not the full generative classifier. After projection one may still run logistic regression or k-NN.

这里的 LDA 指找利于分开的线性特征，不是完整生成式分类器。投影后仍可接逻辑回归或 k-NN。

---

## Practice / 练习

If two classes share the same mean, what is $S_{B}$ and what happens to $J$?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$S_{B}=0$, so $J=0$ for every $\mathbf{u}$; Fisher LDA finds no useful direction from means alone.

$S_{B}=0$，$J$ 恒为 $0$；仅靠均值无法找到有用方向。

</details>
