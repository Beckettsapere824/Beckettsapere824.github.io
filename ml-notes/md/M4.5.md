# M4.5 LDA – Generalized Eigenvalue Problem

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.4 Fisher criterion](M4.4.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

How do we actually maximise $J(\mathbf{u})=(\mathbf{u}^{\top}S_{B}\mathbf{u})/(\mathbf{u}^{\top}S_{W}\mathbf{u})$?

如何真正最大化 $J(\mathbf{u})$？

---

## Step 1: Generalized eigen-equation / 第一步：广义特征方程

Critical points satisfy

驻点满足

$$
S_{B}\mathbf{u}=\lambda S_{W}\mathbf{u}.
$$

If $S_{W}$ is invertible this is an ordinary eigenproblem for $S_{W}^{-1}S_{B}$. The largest $\lambda$ gives the best Fisher direction.

$S_{W}$ 可逆时化为 $S_{W}^{-1}S_{B}$ 的普通特征问题。最大 $\lambda$ 对应最佳 Fisher 方向。

---

## Step 2: Rank limit / 第二步：秩的限制

$\mathrm{rank}(S_{B})\le C-1$, so there are at most **$C-1$** useful LDA directions. For binary classification, one direction suffices.

$\mathrm{rank}(S_{B})\le C-1$，故最多 **$C-1$** 个有用 LDA 方向。二分类只需一个方向。

---

## Step 3: Small-sample fix / 第三步：小样本修正

When $d\gg n$, $S_{W}$ is singular. Common recipe: **PCA first** (reduce to rank-safe dimension), then LDA—the Fisherfaces pipeline for recognition.

$d\gg n$ 时 $S_{W}$ 奇异。常用：**先 PCA** 再 LDA——识别中的 Fisherfaces 流水线。

---

## Practice / 练习

For $C=10$ face identities, how many LDA axes can you extract at most?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

At most $9=C-1$.

最多 $9=C-1$。

</details>
