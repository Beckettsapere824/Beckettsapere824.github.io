# M5.1 k-Means Objective & Lloyd Algorithm

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.1 Vectors](M0.1.md), [M0.2 Distances](M0.2.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

No labels. We want to partition points into $k$ groups so that points in a group are close to a shared **centre**.

没有标签。希望把点分成 $k$ 组，使组内点靠近一个共享 **中心**。

---

## Step 1: Objective / 第一步：目标

$$
J(\mathbf{c}_{1},\ldots,\mathbf{c}_{k},\text{assignments})
=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lVert\mathbf{x}_{i}-\mathbf{c}_{z_{i}}\rVert_{2}^{2},
$$

where $z_{i}\in\{1,\ldots,k\}$ is the cluster index of point $i$. Tight clusters ⇒ small $J$.

$z_{i}$ 是点 $i$ 所属簇。簇越紧 $J$ 越小。

---

## Step 2: Lloyd’s alternating steps / 第二步：Lloyd 交替两步

1. **Assignment:** fix centres, set $z_{i}=\arg\min_{j}\lVert\mathbf{x}_{i}-\mathbf{c}_{j}\rVert_{2}$.  
2. **Update:** fix assignments, set $\mathbf{c}_{j}$ to the mean of points with $z_{i}=j$.

1. **归属：** 固定中心，点归最近中心。  
2. **更新：** 固定归属，中心取该簇均值。

Each step cannot increase $J$ (assignment picks the best centre; the mean minimises sum of squared distances). With finitely many partitions, the algorithm stops at a **local** minimum.

每一步都不增 $J$（归属选最优中心；均值最小化平方距离和）。划分有限，故停在 **局部** 最小。

---

## Step 3: What can go wrong / 第三步：会出什么问题

Different random starts → different local minima. Feature scales distort Euclidean distance—standardise first. Empty clusters can appear if a centre receives no points.

不同随机起点 → 不同局部最优。特征尺度扭曲欧氏距离——先标准化。中心可能分不到点而成空簇。

---

## Practice / 练习

Why is the mean the optimal centre for a fixed set of points under squared Euclidean loss?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$\nabla_{\mathbf{c}}\sum_{i}\lVert\mathbf{x}_{i}-\mathbf{c}\rVert_{2}^{2}=0$ gives $\mathbf{c}=\tfrac{1}{m}\sum_{i}\mathbf{x}_{i}$.

对 $\mathbf{c}$ 求导为零即得均值。

</details>
