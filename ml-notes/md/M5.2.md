# M5.2 k-Means++ Initialization & Practical Issues

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.1 k-means](M5.1.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Random centres often start crowded in one region and miss other modes. **k-means++** spreads the initial centres on purpose.

随机中心常挤在一处、错过其他峰。**k-means++** 有意把初始中心拉开。

---

## Step 1: $D^{2}$ sampling / 第一步：$D^{2}$ 抽样

1. Choose the first centre uniformly from the data.  
2. For each remaining centre, sample point $\mathbf{x}$ with probability proportional to $D(\mathbf{x})^{2}$, where $D(\mathbf{x})$ is the distance to the **nearest** centre already chosen.  
3. Run ordinary Lloyd iterations.

1. 第一个中心均匀抽。  
2. 之后每个点按到 **已有最近中心** 距离的平方成比例被抽中。  
3. 再跑普通 Lloyd。

Far-away points are more likely to become centres → better coverage. In expectation the initial cost is an $O(\log k)$ approximation of the optimal k-means cost.

远处的点更易成为中心 → 覆盖更好。期望初始代价是最优的 $O(\log k)$ 近似。

---

## Step 2: Practical checklist / 第二步：实务清单

- **Scale features** before clustering.  
- **Try several restarts** even with k-means++.  
- **Choose $k$** with domain knowledge, elbow of $J(k)$, or validity indices (M5.4)—not only training $J$.  
- **Empty cluster:** re-seed from a far point or drop that centre.

- 先缩放特征。  
- 即使 k-means++ 也多开几次。  
- 用领域知识 / 肘部 / 有效性指标选 $k$。  
- 空簇：从远处重播或丢掉该中心。

---

## Practice / 练习

In one sentence, why does sampling proportional to $D^{2}$ help?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

It prefers points that are poorly explained by current centres, so new centres land in uncovered regions.

它偏好当前中心解释不好的点，使新中心落在未覆盖区域。

</details>
