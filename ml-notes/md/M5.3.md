# M5.3 Hierarchical Agglomerative Clustering & Linkages

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.1](M0.1.md), [M0.2](M0.2.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

k-means gives a flat partition and needs $k$ up front. Hierarchical clustering builds a **tree** of merges so you can cut at any level.

k-means 是扁平划分且要预先给 $k$。层次聚类建一棵 **合并树**，可在任意高度切开。

---

## Step 1: Agglomerative process / 第一步：凝聚过程

Start with each point as its own cluster. Repeatedly merge the two clusters that are **closest** under a chosen linkage, until one cluster remains. The history is a dendrogram.

每点一簇起；按选定 linkage 不断合并最近两簇，直到只剩一簇。历史记录成树状图。

---

## Step 2: Linkage definitions / 第二步：linkage 定义

| Linkage | Distance between clusters $A,B$ |
|---------|----------------------------------|
| Single | $\min_{a\in A,b\in B}d(a,b)$ |
| Complete | $\max_{a\in A,b\in B}d(a,b)$ |
| Average | mean of all cross-pair distances |
| Ward | increase in total within-cluster variance after merge |

Single linkage follows thin bridges (chaining). Complete prefers compact balls. Ward is often a strong default when clusters are roughly spherical.

单链易被细桥连接；全链偏爱紧球；近似球形簇时 Ward 常是稳妥默认。

---

## Step 3: Reading the dendrogram / 第三步：读树状图

Vertical axis = merge distance. A horizontal cut defines a partition. Unlike k-means, you see which groups merged early vs late.

纵轴 = 合并距离。水平一刀定义划分。与 k-means 不同，能看到谁早合、谁晚合。

---

## Practice / 练习

Name one failure mode of single linkage.

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

A few noisy points can form a bridge between two real groups so they merge too early (chaining).

少量噪声可在两真实群之间搭桥，导致过早合并（链状效应）。

</details>
