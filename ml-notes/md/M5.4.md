# M5.4 Cluster Validity Indices

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.1 k-means](M5.1.md), [M5.3 Hierarchical](M5.3.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Clustering always returns *some* partition. Is it meaningful? Is $k$ reasonable? Indices give **reference scores**—not absolute truth.

聚类总会返回某个划分。有意义吗？$k$ 合理吗？指标给出 **参考分数**——不是绝对真理。

---

## Step 1: Internal indices (no labels) / 第一步：内部指标（无标签）

- **Silhouette** of a point: compare average distance to its own cluster vs the nearest other cluster. Near $+1$: well matched; near $0$: boundary; negative: likely wrong cluster.  
- **Davies–Bouldin:** average “similarity” of each cluster to its most similar peer (lower is better).

- **轮廓系数：** 比较到本簇平均距离与到最近他簇距离。近 $+1$ 好；近 $0$ 在边界；负值可能归错。  
- **Davies–Bouldin：** 各簇与最相似簇的平均“像度”（越小越好）。

---

## Step 2: External indices (with ground truth) / 第二步：外部指标（有真值）

- **Adjusted Rand Index (ARI):** agreement between two partitions, corrected for chance (1 = perfect, 0 ≈ random).  
- **Normalised Mutual Information (NMI):** shared information between cluster labels and true labels, scaled to $[0,1]$.

- **ARI：** 两划分一致性，校正偶然（1 完美，0 ≈ 随机）。  
- **NMI：** 簇标签与真标签的归一化互信息。

---

## Step 3: Use with care / 第三步：谨慎使用

Optimising silhouette alone can favour overly round, equally sized clusters. Always pair scores with a plot when $d$ is small enough. Domain checks beat leaderboard chasing.

只优化轮廓可能偏向过圆、等大的簇。维数允许时分数要配合图。领域检查优于刷榜。

---

## Practice / 练习

When would you prefer an external index over silhouette?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

When a reliable ground-truth labeling exists (benchmark data, expert tags) and you want to measure recovery of that labeling.

有可靠真值（基准数据、专家标注）且目标是恢复该标注时。

</details>
