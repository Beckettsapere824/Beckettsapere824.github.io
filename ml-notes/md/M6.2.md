# M6.2 Bagging (Bootstrap Aggregating)

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M6.1 Voting](M6.1.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

We want diverse models but only one dataset. **Bootstrap** samples create many slightly different training sets; aggregating the resulting models mainly **reduces variance**.

想要多样的模型却只有一份数据。**自助** 采样制造多份略有差异的训练集；聚合后的模型主要 **降方差**。

---

## Step 1: Bootstrap sample / 第一步：自助样本

Draw $n$ indices **with replacement** from $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. About $63\%$ of unique points appear; the rest are left out (out-of-bag).

有放回抽 $n$ 个下标。约 $63\%$ 的点出现；其余为袋外样本。

---

## Step 2: Bagging procedure / 第二步：Bagging 流程

1. For $t=1,\ldots,T$: draw bootstrap set $D_{t}$, train base learner $h_{t}$.  
2. Aggregate by majority vote (classification) or averaging (regression).

1. 对 $t=1,\ldots,T$：抽 $D_{t}$，训练 $h_{t}$。  
2. 分类多数票 / 回归取平均。

Base learners are trained **independently** → easy to parallelise.

基学习器 **独立** 训练 → 易并行。

---

## Step 3: When it helps / 第三步：何时有用

High-variance bases (deep trees) benefit most: averaging noisy fits stabilises predictions. Already-stable models gain little. **Random forests** = bagging trees + random feature subsets at each split for extra diversity.

高方差基学习器（深树）受益最大。已很稳的模型收益有限。**随机森林** = 对树做 Bagging + 每次分裂随机特征子集以增加多样性。

---

## Practice / 练习

Does bagging primarily target bias or variance? Why?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Variance—averaging many noisy but roughly unbiased estimators reduces variance; bias of each tree stays similar.

方差——平均多个嘈杂但大致无偏的估计降方差；每棵树的偏差大体不变。

</details>
