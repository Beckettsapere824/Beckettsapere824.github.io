# M6.3 Boosting Idea & AdaBoost Derivation

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M6.1 Voting](M6.1.md), [M2.7 Logistic / losses](M2.7.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Bagging treats every training point equally and trains models in parallel. **Boosting** trains models **sequentially**, forcing each new model to focus on points the current ensemble still gets wrong—mainly reducing **bias** of weak learners.

Bagging 平等对待样本并并行训练。**Boosting** **串行** 训练，强迫新模型关注当前集成仍分错的点——主要降低弱学习器的 **偏差**。

---

## Step 1: Weak learners / 第一步：弱学习器

A weak learner needs only to beat random guessing slightly (error rate $\varepsilon<1/2$ for binary labels $\pm 1$). Stumps (depth-1 trees) are classic weak learners for AdaBoost.

弱学习器只需略好于乱猜（二分类 $\pm 1$ 时错误率 $\varepsilon<1/2$）。决策桩是 AdaBoost 的经典弱学习器。

---

## Step 2: One AdaBoost round / 第二步：一轮 AdaBoost

Weights $w_{i}$ on samples (start uniform). Train $h_{t}$ to minimise weighted error

样本权重 $w_{i}$（初始均匀）。训练 $h_{t}$ 使加权错误

$$
\varepsilon_{t}=\sum_{i:h_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{i})\neq y_{i}}w_{i}
$$

(normalised so $\sum_{i}w_{i}=1$). Set

（权重已归一）。令

$$
\alpha_{t}=\tfrac{1}{2}\ln\frac{1-\varepsilon_{t}}{\varepsilon_{t}}.
$$

Update weights: increase on mistakes, decrease on correct predictions

更新权重：错的升权，对的降权

$$
w_{i}\leftarrow w_{i}\exp\bigl(-\alpha_{t}y_{i}h_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{i})\bigr),
$$

then renormalise. Final predictor: $\mathrm{sign}\bigl(\sum_{t}\alpha_{t}h_{t}(\mathbf{x})\bigr)$.

再归一化。最终预测：$\mathrm{sign}\bigl(\sum_{t}\alpha_{t}h_{t}(\mathbf{x})\bigr)$。

---

## Step 3: Where $\alpha_{t}$ comes from / 第三步：$\alpha_{t}$ 从哪来

AdaBoost can be seen as coordinate descent on exponential loss $\sum_{i}\exp\bigl(-y_{i}F(\mathbf{x}_{i})\bigr)$ with $F=\sum_{t}\alpha_{t}h_{t}$. Fixing previous terms and optimising the next pair $(h_{t},\alpha_{t})$ yields exactly $\alpha_{t}=\tfrac{1}{2}\ln\frac{1-\varepsilon_{t}}{\varepsilon_{t}}$.

可视为对指数损失 $\sum_{i}\exp(-y_{i}F(\mathbf{x}_{i}))$ 的坐标下降。固定已有项、优化下一对 $(h_{t},\alpha_{t})$ 即得该 $\alpha_{t}$。

---

## Step 4: Overfitting note / 第四步：过拟合提示

Boosting often resists overfitting longer than one might expect, but it is **not** immune—especially with noisy labels or very many rounds. Validate $T$.

Boosting 常比预期更耐过拟合，但 **并非** 免疫——标签噪声或多轮时仍可能。用验证集选 $T$。

---

## Practice / 练习

If $\varepsilon_{t}=0.25$, compute $\alpha_{t}$. Does a smaller $\varepsilon_{t}$ give a larger $\alpha_{t}$?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$\alpha_{t}=\tfrac{1}{2}\ln(0.75/0.25)=\tfrac{1}{2}\ln 3\approx 0.549$. Yes—smaller error → larger weight on that weak learner.

$\alpha_{t}\approx 0.549$。错误率越小，该弱学习器权重越大。

</details>
