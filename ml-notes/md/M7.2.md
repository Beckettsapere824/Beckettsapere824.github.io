# M7.2 Gini Impurity

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M7.1 Entropy](M7.1.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Entropy needs logarithms. **Gini impurity** is a cheaper impurity score with the same qualitative behaviour; CART uses it by default.

熵要算对数。**Gini 不纯度** 更便宜且定性行为相近；CART 默认使用。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$$
G=1-\sum_{c=1}^{C}p_{c}^{2}.
$$

Pure: $G=0$. Two classes at $1/2$ each: $G=1/2$. Interpretable as the probability that two randomly drawn labels from the node disagree (with replacement).

纯：$G=0$。两类各半：$G=1/2$。可理解为有放回抽两个标签不同的概率。

---

## Step 2: Gini gain / 第二步：Gini 增益

Same structure as information gain:

与信息增益同结构：

$$
\Delta G=G(S)-\sum_{j}\frac{|S_{j}|}{|S|}G(S_{j}).
$$

Choose the split maximising $\Delta G$. In practice, trees from Gini vs entropy are often similar.

选使 $\Delta G$ 最大的分裂。实务中 Gini 与熵选出的树往往接近。

---

## Practice / 练习

Gini for $(A,A,B,B,B,B)$?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$p_{A}=1/3$, $p_{B}=2/3$, $G=1-(1/9+4/9)=4/9\approx 0.44$.

</details>
