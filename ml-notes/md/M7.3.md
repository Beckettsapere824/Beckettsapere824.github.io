# M7.3 CART / ID3 Recursive Partitioning

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M7.1 Entropy](M7.1.md), [M7.2 Gini](M7.2.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Impurity scores rate one split. A **tree** is grown by applying that rule recursively until a stopping condition fires.

不纯度给一次分裂打分。**树** 通过递归应用该规则长出，直到触发停止条件。

---

## Step 1: Grow step / 第一步：生长一步

At a node:

1. Enumerate candidate splits (for continuous features: sort values, try thresholds between adjacent distinct values).  
2. Score each by IG or $\Delta G$.  
3. Apply the best split; recurse on children.

在节点上：枚举候选分裂（连续特征：排序后试相邻中点）→ 用 IG 或 $\Delta G$ 打分 → 选最优并递归。

CART uses **binary** splits; ID3/C4.5 may branch multiway on categorical features.

CART 用 **二分**；ID3/C4.5 可对类别特征多路分支。

---

## Step 2: Stopping / 第二步：停止

Typical rules: node is pure; fewer than $n_{\min}$ samples; depth $\ge d_{\max}$; no split improves impurity enough.

常见规则：已纯；样本少于 $n_{\min}$；深度 $\ge d_{\max}$；没有足够改善的分裂。

---

## Step 3: Geometry / 第三步：几何

Axis-aligned cuts tile the space into rectangles (or boxes in higher $d$). Easy to explain (“if age $>30$ and income $\le x$…”), but deep trees memorise noise.

轴对齐切割把空间切成矩形。易解释，但深树会背噪声。

---

## Practice / 练习

Why sort continuous features once per node rather than testing every real threshold?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Only thresholds between adjacent distinct values change the partition; sorting makes those candidates $O(n)$ after $O(n\log n)$ sort.

只有相邻不同值之间的阈值会改变划分；排序后候选是 $O(n)$ 级。

</details>
