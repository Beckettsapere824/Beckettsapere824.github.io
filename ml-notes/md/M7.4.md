# M7.4 Overfitting, Pruning, Tree Ensembles Preview

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M7.3 CART](M7.3.md), [M6.2 Bagging](M6.2.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

A fully grown tree can reach zero training error and still fail on new data. **Pruning** cuts unreliable branches; **ensembles** average many trees.

长满的树可以训练误差为零，仍在新数据上失败。**剪枝** 砍掉不可靠的枝；**集成** 平均许多树。

---

## Step 1: Pre- vs post-pruning / 第一步：预剪枝与后剪枝

- **Pre-pruning:** stop early (depth, min leaf size, min gain). Fast, may underfit.  
- **Post-pruning:** grow full tree, then remove splits that do not help on validation data (e.g. cost-complexity pruning: minimise $\mathrm{error}+\alpha|\mathrm{leaves}|$).

- **预剪枝：** 提前停（深度、叶大小、最小增益）。快，可能欠拟合。  
- **后剪枝：** 先长满再按验证集删分裂（如代价复杂度：最小化 $\mathrm{error}+\alpha|\mathrm{leaves}|$）。

---

## Step 2: Trees in ensembles / 第二步：树与集成

Single deep trees have **high variance**. Bagging many trees → **random forests** (extra randomness via feature subsets). Sequentially boosting trees → **gradient boosted trees** (XGBoost-style), focusing on residuals.

单棵深树 **方差高**。Bagging 多树 → **随机森林**。串行提升树 → **梯度提升树**，聚焦残差。

---

## Step 3: Tuning checklist / 第三步：调参清单

Watch validation curves. Do not only tune max depth—also min samples per leaf. Aggressive pruning → underfit; none → overfit.

看验证曲线。不要只调最大深度——也要看叶最小样本。剪太狠欠拟合；不剪过拟合。

---

## Practice / 练习

In one line: why do random forests usually beat one pruned tree on tabular data?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

Averaging many diverse high-variance trees reduces variance while keeping low bias of flexible bases.

平均许多多样的高方差树以降方差，同时保留灵活基学习器的低偏差。

</details>
