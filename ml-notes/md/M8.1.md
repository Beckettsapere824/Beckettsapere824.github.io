# M8.1 Multilayer Perceptron & Activation Functions

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.7 Logistic](M2.7.md), [M0.8 Matrix calculus](M0.8.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Linear models (even with hand-crafted $\phi$) have limited shape. We want **learnable** nonlinear maps built by stacking simple pieces.

线性模型（即使手工 $\phi$）形状有限。希望通过堆叠简单部件得到 **可学习** 的非线性映射。

---

## Step 1: One layer / 第一步：一层

$$
\mathbf{z}=W^{\top}\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{b},
\qquad
\mathbf{h}=\varphi(\mathbf{z})
$$

elementwise. $W,\mathbf{b}$ are parameters; $\varphi$ is a fixed nonlinearity (**activation**).

逐元素激活。$W,\mathbf{b}$ 是参数；$\varphi$ 是固定非线性（**激活**）。

---

## Step 2: Why nonlinearity? / 第二步：为何要非线性？

If $\varphi$ is the identity, stacking layers collapses to one linear map. Activations are what create expressive depth.

若 $\varphi$ 是恒等，多层叠完仍是一个线性映射。激活才是深度表达力的来源。

---

## Step 3: Common activations / 第三步：常用激活

| $\varphi$ | Formula | Note |
|-----------|---------|------|
| Sigmoid | $1/(1+e^{-z})$ | saturates; rare in deep hidden layers |
| Tanh | $\tanh z$ | zero-centred; still saturates |
| ReLU | $\max(0,z)$ | default hidden unit; sparse, cheap gradients |

Sigmoid still appears at binary outputs; softmax (M2.8) at multiclass outputs.

Sigmoid 仍用于二元输出；多类输出用 softmax（M2.8）。

---

## Step 4: Width vs depth / 第四步：宽度与深度

A single wide hidden layer can approximate continuous functions on compact sets (universal approximation). Deeper stacks often need fewer parameters for the same accuracy and build hierarchical features.

单层足够宽可逼近紧集上的连续函数。更深往往更省参数，并形成层次特征。

---

## Practice / 练习

What happens to a two-layer net if every activation is replaced by the identity?

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

It becomes equivalent to a single linear (affine) map; depth adds no new function class.

等价于单个仿射映射；深度不再扩大函数类。

</details>
