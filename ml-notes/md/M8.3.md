# M8.3 Backpropagation – Chain Rule on Graphs

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.2 Graph](M8.2.md), [M0.8 Matrix calculus](M0.8.md)

---

## What problem? / 要解决什么？

Training needs $\partial J/\partial W$ for every weight. Forward-mode differentiation would restart per parameter. **Reverse-mode** AD (backprop) starts from the scalar loss and walks the graph backward once.

训练需要每个权重的 $\partial J/\partial W$。前向模式要对每个参数重来。**反向模式** 自动微分（反传）从标量损失出发沿图走一遍。

---

## Step 1: Local chain rule / 第一步：局部链式法则

If node $v$ computed $v=f(u)$, and we already know $\bar{v}=\partial J/\partial v$, then

若节点 $v=f(u)$ 且已知 $\bar{v}=\partial J/\partial v$，则

$$
\bar{u}=\bar{v}\,\frac{\partial f}{\partial u}.
$$

(Matrix layouts need matching transposes—see M0.8.)

（矩阵布局要注意转置——见 M0.8。）

---

## Step 2: Linear + activation / 第二步：线性 + 激活

For $Z=XW$, $H=\varphi(Z)$, $J$ downstream:

对 $Z=XW$，$H=\varphi(Z)$，下游损失 $J$：

$$
\bar{Z}=\bar{H}\odot\varphi'(Z),
\qquad
\bar{W}=X^{\top}\bar{Z},
\qquad
\bar{X}=\bar{Z}W^{\top}.
$$

($\odot$ = elementwise product.) Same pattern as the logistic gradient $X^{\top}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{y})$ when $\varphi$ and loss combine that way.

$\odot$ 为逐元素积。与逻辑回归梯度 $X^{\top}(\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{y})$ 同形。

---

## Step 3: Cost / 第三步：代价

One backward pass costs on the same order as one forward pass—not proportional to the number of parameters. That is why deep nets with millions of weights are trainable.

一次反向与一次前向同量级——不与参数个数成正比。因此百万权重的网络仍可训练。

---

## Practice / 练习

For scalar $z=wx$, $h=\mathrm{ReLU}(z)$, $J=\tfrac12(h-y)^{2}$ with $w=2,x=1,y=0$, compute $\partial J/\partial w$.

<details>
<summary>Answer / 答案</summary>

$z=2$, $h=2$, $\partial J/\partial h=h-y=2$, $\varphi'(z)=1$, $\partial J/\partial z=2$, $\partial J/\partial w=x\cdot 2=2$.

$\partial J/\partial w=2$。

</details>
